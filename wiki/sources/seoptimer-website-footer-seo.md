# Website Footer SEO Best Practices (SEOptimer)

**Citation:** Kang, Jay. "Website Footer SEO Best Practices."
SEOptimer, Website Design. Published 2019-10-01.
https://www.seoptimer.com/blog/website-footer-seo/

The oldest of the three footer sources ingested together (2019) and the
most design/UX-oriented. Ingested alongside
[[growth-memo-show-me-your-footer]] and
[[heydaymarketing-footer-optimization]].

## Key takeaways

- **Seven reasons footers matter**: internal link creation, a final
  action prompt, easier navigation (especially on mobile, where
  returning to the top nav means a long scroll), links to brand
  properties, contact/legal information, link-equity distribution, and
  credibility/trust signals.
- **"No dead-end pages"** — the footer guarantees every page offers an
  onward path, which is a crawl-graph benefit as well as a UX one.
- **Must-have elements**: copyright notice (auto-updated in code rather
  than hand-edited), contact information (address, phone, optionally an
  inline contact form; phone-click tracking as a measurable signal), and
  a call to action.
- **One CTA, and make it generic.** Page-specific CTAs (a demo link on
  the product page) don't belong in a footer that renders sitewide; two
  competing CTAs dilute both. The CTA should be visually prominent and
  completable in the footer itself (e.g. an inline signup form).
- **At least three sections** (company / services / contact) — the
  section structure itself communicates how the site is organized.
- **Simplicity over volume.** Explicitly recommends restraint: the
  worked negative example is a footer listing 10 office addresses; above
  ~3 locations, link to a dedicated locations page instead (WeWork given
  as the positive example). **This directly conflicts with
  [[growth-memo-show-me-your-footer]]'s "fat but not obese"** position.
- **Link only the most important pages** — listing sections isn't
  enough; the links should genuinely route users somewhere useful.
- **Brand consistency**: same theme colors, logo and font family as the
  rest of the site; use type-size contrast (larger CTA, smaller
  copyright) to create hierarchy.
- **Trust/commerce signals**: SSL/security certificate display and
  accepted payment methods for ecommerce; industry credentials (BBB
  accreditation, Google certifications, GSA certifications) where
  relevant. Overlaps with the Trust leg of [[e-e-a-t-and-page-quality]].
- **Usability affordances**: a back-to-top button for long-form pages,
  and optionally live chat — with attention to overlap/collision between
  fixed elements.

## Caveats

- **2019 vintage.** Predates the 2023 Mueller statement on footer link
  weight, the AI-search era, and much of the current internal-linking
  literature. Nothing in it is falsified by age, but its SEO claims are
  asserted rather than sourced.
- **No citations or data.** Link-equity and crawl claims are stated as
  received wisdom; the article's evidence is entirely screenshot
  examples.
- The link-equity explanation is loosely worded (it describes equity
  flowing *to* the page containing the footer link rather than from it).
