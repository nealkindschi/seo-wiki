Semrush Team, "Niche Driven SEO Guide: Definition, Benefits, and
Strategy Explained" (Semrush Blog, 2023-11-14; page since refreshed,
update date not shown). Raw copy is a fetch-tool paraphrase, not
verbatim.

## Key takeaways

- Defines niche-driven SEO as optimizing for keywords tied to a specific
  market segment: less competition than general SEO, but niche
  businesses must work harder to stand out.
- Seven-step strategy: audience research (GA4, CRM, surveys), site
  health, long-tail keyword selection, high-value content matching
  intent, relationship/link building, metric tracking, competitor
  monitoring. Generic and tool-promotional (Semrush features), with no
  original data.
- Examples of niche content targeting: Pipedrive ranking for
  "confirmation email template"; Freetrain targeting problem-based
  queries ("where to keep your phone at the gym").
- Cites third-party stats (Marketing Insider Group: paid + social under
  10% of B2B traffic/leads/sales; Backlinko: top pages have more
  backlinks) without original methodology here.
