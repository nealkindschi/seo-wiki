Matt Diggity (Diggity Marketing) with Empire Flippers data, "What a
Data Dump From Empire Flippers Told Us About the Best Niches for
Affiliate Marketing" (listed 2025-08-06; images date from 2021). Raw
copy is a fetch-tool paraphrase. **Weak evidence**: see caveats.

## Key takeaways

- Sample: 300+ affiliate, display-ad, and Amazon Associate sites sold
  through Empire Flippers since 2019.
- Averages by niche (monthly revenue): News & Education ~$8,439 (top
  after outlier adjustment), Affiliate-model sites ~$5,540, Medical
  ~$4,591, Pet Care ~$4,319, Food & Beverages ~$4,039, Baking lowest at
  ~$773.
- ROI leaders: Medical ~$33 revenue per page (content ROI), Office
  ~$5.61 per backlink (link ROI), Survival & Security ~$2,860/hour (time
  ROI, outlier-driven, self-reported hours).
- Affiliate sites earned roughly 3x more per backlink than other
  monetization models.
- Diminishing returns: past a few hundred backlinks (most sites
  250-750) and past ~250 pages (most sites 25-250).
- **Caveats**: only *sold* sites (survivorship bias); small per-niche
  samples with outliers removed ad hoc; time data self-reported;
  promotional for Diggity Marketing and Empire Flippers; the article's
  quick summary (Home & Kitchen, Fitness & Sports, Health & Personal
  Care) disagrees with its body emphasis (Medical).
