## Key takeaways

- Niche keywords = highly specific long-tail terms with little
  competition and high relevance ("carbon plate running shoe for heel
  striker" vs. "sports shoes").
- Five steps: define audience (GA4 demographics), research the market
  (publications, podcasts, recurring topics), evaluate direct
  competitors (Keyword Gap), browse communities (Reddit, Quora,
  Facebook groups), compile and validate.
- Practical filters: Keyword Magic Tool KD "Easy"/0-49%; Keyword Gap
  "Missing"/"Untapped" tabs with volume 11-100; Google Keyword Planner
  sorted low-to-high volume; Autocomplete with qualifiers; GSC queries
  that earn impressions without deliberate targeting.
- Pre-AI-search (2023): no prompt or fan-out coverage. Superseded in
  framing by [[semrush-keyword-research-2026]], which keeps the same
  0-49% difficulty heuristic.
