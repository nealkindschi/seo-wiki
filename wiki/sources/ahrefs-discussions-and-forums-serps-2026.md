# Reddit Appears in 83.9% of Google's Discussions and Forums Results (Ahrefs)

**Citation:** Allsopp, Glen (contributor: Xibeijia Guan; reviewed by
Ryan Law). "Analysis: Reddit Appears in 83.9% of Google's Discussions
and Forums Results." Ahrefs Blog. Published 2026-08-27.
https://ahrefs.com/blog/discussions-and-forums-serps/
(Methodology: 145M US Google SERPs sampled via Ahrefs' proprietary web
index; the Discussions and Forums feature appeared in 16.93M of them
(11.7%); domain registration dates cross-referenced via archive.org;
also sampled across dozens of non-US countries.)

## Key takeaways

- **Reddit dominates the Discussions and Forums feature**: 83.9% of
  worldwide appearances, 87.8% in the US specifically, dominant in 34
  countries. Reddit + Facebook + Quora = 76.75% of all discussion
  links; top 10 domains = 84.87%; top 1,000 domains = 96.81%.
- **Facebook overtook Quora for the #2 spot in January 2026** (now 38.3%
  of appearances vs. Quora), driven entirely by public Facebook Groups
  (a format that has existed since Oct 2010).
- **Domain authority barely matters**: only a moderate correlation
  (ρ=0.38) between Domain Rating and prominence; a 3-year-11-month-old
  domain (XDAforums.com) ranks prominently despite the average
  prominent domain being 21 years 8 months old.
- **No correlation between backlinks to a specific thread and its
  ranking** — Google appears to rank discussion threads on topical
  relevance/engagement signals, not link signals, for this feature.
- **Concentration risk at the URL level**: 27 domains rely on a single
  URL for 10%+ of their appearances; 768 of the top 1,000 domains
  depend on one URL for 1%+ of their presence — a handful of viral
  threads carry outsized SERP weight.
- **Feature prevalence scales with query length and informational
  intent**: 98.7% of appearances are on informational queries; the
  feature appears in 11.7% of all US SERPs but is ~3x more frequent at
  8-word queries than 1-word queries.
- **Geographic spread**: most prevalent in US (11.7%), Canada (10.7%),
  Philippines, Australia, UK; Quora is India's #2 platform, Facebook is
  South Africa's #2.
