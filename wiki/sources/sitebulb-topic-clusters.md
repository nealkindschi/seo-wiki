Maret Reutelingsperger, "The Importance of Topic Clusters" (Sitebulb,
updated 2025-03-31).

## Key takeaways

- Adds a third page tier missing from the other two topic-cluster
  sources: **target pages** — decision-stage, longer-tail, conversion-
  focused pages (product/service pages) sitting alongside pillar and
  cluster pages, mapped to the buyer journey's awareness → consideration
  → decision stages.
- Practical build tactics distinct from the other sources: pivot
  straight from *existing* keyword-research spreadsheets (tab names
  become pillar topics) rather than starting from scratch; use folder-
  based URL hierarchy (`example.com/topic/cluster/`) for technical
  clarity; source real questions from support teams and surveys (not
  just keyword tools) to keep clusters genuinely useful rather than
  link-stuffed.
- Recommends regular internal-link auditing via crawl-visualization
  tooling (its own product, Sitebulb) to catch dead/irrelevant
  cluster links over time — clusters decay if not maintained.
- Ties clusters explicitly to Google's E-E-A-T expansion and Helpful
  Content Updates as the underlying algorithmic rationale.
