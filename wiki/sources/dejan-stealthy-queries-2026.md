# Finding High-Value Converting Queries That Mainstream Tools Miss (DEJAN)

Petrovic, D. / DEJAN AI (2026-10-12). dejan.ai/blog/stealthy-queries/.

**Nature of source**: practitioner essay plus a crowdsourced LinkedIn
thread; one anecdotal GSC example. No controlled data.

## Key takeaways

1. **Zero-volume ≠ zero demand.** A paper-title query showed zero volume
   in a keyword tool but produced 1,020 impressions / 37 clicks (avg
   pos 4.3, 3.6% CTR) in one month in GSC, plus AI Overview/chat
   visibility — and that's one variant of many fragments.
2. **Stealth benefit**: third-party traffic estimators see such sites
   as near-zero traffic, so competitors ignore them.
3. **Tactic**: jump early on trending research/papers/data to be the
   primary target during the spike.
4. **Raw human-query sources**: internal site search (author's
   favourite), support transcripts, chatbot sessions; quote requests
   and CRM notes joined to closed deals (problem + constraint phrasing,
   e.g. "furnace shuts off after five minutes"); sales calls and lead
   forms; Reddit/Telegram/YouTube comments/reviews; local speech.
5. **Discovery frameworks**: buyer interviews/mystery shopping
   (implementer vs. approver personas), backwards journey mapping from
   purchase, Messy Middle prompting, jobs-to-be-done statements, broad
   match Google Ads with heavy negatives to surface converting
   unrecorded queries.
6. **Synthetic expansion caveat**: embedding-space traversal yields
   candidates, but they're probabilistic — still need proof real people
   use the phrasing.
7. **Proposed pipeline**: collect raw inputs → LLM intent classification
   → cluster into canonical variants → entity graph of topics, problems,
   offerings → content + internal links.
