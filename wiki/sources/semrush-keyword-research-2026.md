Chris Hanna (contributors: Faizan Ali, Alex Lindley), "How to Do
Keyword Research in 2026" (Semrush Blog, published 2026-05-05).

## Key takeaways

- Names three structural shifts in keyword research: volume-first →
  business-value-first (AI features increasingly answer high-volume
  queries before a click happens), Google-only → search-everywhere
  (Reddit, Amazon, ChatGPT), and query-matching → prompt research
  (longer, conversational AI-style prompts vs. short search queries).
- Distinct discovery method not covered by
  [[keyword-mapping-and-cannibalization]]'s existing process: **mining
  first-party data** — sales-call questions/objections, support-ticket
  recurring issues, onboarding-conversation confusion points — as a
  zero-cost, high-signal keyword source competitors can't easily
  replicate.
- Concrete AI-workflow tactic: paste raw social/forum comment text
  (Reddit, YouTube comments, TikTok) into ChatGPT/Claude with a
  targeted extraction prompt to surface unanswered questions and
  zero-volume-but-real-demand keyword opportunities — a practical,
  reusable prompt pattern.
- Explicitly ties long-tail keyword targeting to AI **query fan-out**
  behavior (AI systems running multiple related sub-searches per
  prompt to build a comprehensive answer) — long-tail keywords align
  with the sub-queries fan-out generates, giving long-tail content a
  second visibility channel beyond direct search.
- 6-dimension prioritization framework (conversion potential, search
  volume, click potential, real-world demand signals, trend analysis,
  attainability) is a more AI-search-aware evolution of the volume/
  difficulty/intent framing already used in
  [[keyword-mapping-and-cannibalization]] and
  [[riseatseven-keyword-mapping-guide]].
