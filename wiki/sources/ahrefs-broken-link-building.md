Joshua Hardwick, "Broken Link Building: The Complete Guide" (Ahrefs
Blog, published 2022-05-23).

## Key takeaways

- Broken link building ranks as the **5th most popular** link-building
  tactic among 250+ surveyed professionals (Aira's state-of-link-
  building report) — a mainstream, not niche, tactic.
- Four prospect-discovery tactics beyond the basic "find dead
  competitor pages" approach: competitor "best by links" 404 analysis,
  topic-wide Content Explorer search for broken pages with 20+
  referring domains regardless of competitor, checking *competitors'*
  own broken **outgoing** links (a prospect list they've effectively
  already vetted), and resource-page mining.
- Key vetting distinction: **"deep links"** (a specific, stated reason
  for linking) vs. **"general links"** (a vague endorsement) — deep
  links tell you exactly what value proposition to lead with in
  outreach; general links need a more generic pitch.
- Core reframe: "Link building tactics are just streamlined ways of
  finding link prospects with a reason for contact" — broken link
  building's real mechanism is that a 404 error *is* the reason for
  contact, not that dead links carry some special SEO property.
- Replacement-content guidance: use the Wayback Machine to see what the
  original dead page actually said, match its structure, then improve
  on the specific points that "deep linkers" referenced — don't build
  a generic replacement and hope it fits every prospect's original
  reason for linking.
