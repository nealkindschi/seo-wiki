Yannick Weiler (contributors: Shannon Willoby, Bartłomiej Barcik), "SEO
for Brand Awareness: A Complete Guide" (Semrush Blog, published
2024-04-11).

## Key takeaways

- Frames SEO and brand awareness as a **reinforcing loop**: better
  search visibility → more brand recognition → more branded searches
  and organic backlinks → further-improved SEO. Distinct from
  [[brand-entity-seo-strategy]]'s focus (making a brand machine-legible
  to Knowledge Graph/AI) — this source is about the traffic/visibility
  mechanics of brand-building through ordinary content SEO.
- Practical tactic: build **long-tail keyword clusters** around a topic
  (not branded terms) to earn visibility even before the brand itself
  has recognition — e.g. ranking for "personalized scented candles" as
  a path to being discovered, rather than depending on people already
  searching the brand name.
- Concrete keyword-difficulty contrast illustrating the long-tail
  advantage: "candles" KD 61% (hard) vs. "birthday candles" KD 26%
  (easier) — same core topic, very different competitive cost.
- Measurement approach: track **branded search traffic growth** (not
  just rankings) as the brand-awareness KPI, plus total/linked brand
  mentions via a brand-monitoring tool — a leading indicator that
  awareness efforts are working, distinct from conversion metrics.
