## Key takeaways

- Repeats the consensus rationale already on [[glossary-pages-for-seo]]:
  long-tail definition queries, internal linking, topical authority,
  lower bounce. Agrees with it; adds no evidence.
- Adds **non-SEO justifications** the other sources mostly skip:
  support deflection (customers look up terms themselves), internal
  terminology alignment, and brand credibility. These help make the case
  to stakeholders even when search returns are uncertain.
- Two tactics the other sources don't cover: **visuals** (diagrams,
  infographics) in definitions, and **guest posts that link back** to
  glossary terms ([[link-building-outreach-tactics]]).
- Structured CMS fields for definition, synonyms, and related terms
  (via the template). Useful as a data model for keeping entries
  consistent and generating related-term links.
