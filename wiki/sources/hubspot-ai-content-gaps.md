Cassie Wilson Clark, "How to identify content gaps for AI visibility"
(HubSpot Blog, updated 2026-10-23). Raw copy is a fetch-tool paraphrase.
Vendor content (promotes HubSpot AEO and AI Search Grader); no original
data.

## Key takeaways

- Defines an AI visibility content gap: a relevant question where the
  brand is absent from AI answers, lacks supporting citations, or has no
  adequate content. Distinct from SEO gaps: a page can rank and still
  be missing from AI answers.
- Three gap types: **coverage**, **extractability**, **citation-support**.
- Four-step audit: collect prompts (by topic/intent across platforms),
  classify mentions and citations separately, map prompts to existing
  content, prioritize by impact vs. effort.
- Fix order: refresh outdated pages, improve extractability, strengthen
  evidence, create new content only if no suitable page exists, defer
  and document weak gaps.
- Metrics: brand mention presence, citation presence, AI share of voice
  (pick entity- or citation-based and stay consistent); AI referral
  traffic is supporting, not causal, evidence.
- Pitfalls: chasing every missing mention, broad topics over
  high-impact ones, structure over readability, unchecked AI drafts.
