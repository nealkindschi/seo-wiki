2023-07-03).

## Key takeaways

- Broken link building: find dead (404/5xx) links on external sites and
  convince the admin to replace them with a link to your relevant
  content — mutually beneficial (they fix a broken link, you gain a
  backlink), and tends to convert better than cold outreach because it
  offers concrete value up front.
- 5-step process: find your own broken backlinks (Backlink Audit,
  filtered to non-toxic 4xx/5xx) → find competitors' broken backlinks
  (via their backlink profile, or resource-page search operators like
  `intitle:"useful resources"`) → evaluate opportunities (Authority
  Score, contextual relevance, Wayback Machine check of the original
  content, admin-acceptance likelihood) → find a named outreach contact
  (not a generic address) → send a concise, personalized pitch with a
  3-business-day follow-up.
- Tool-specific (Semrush Backlink Audit / Backlinks) walkthrough,
  complementing Ahrefs' tool-agnostic four-step framework in
  [[ahrefs-broken-link-building]].
