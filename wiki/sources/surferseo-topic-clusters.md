Petar Marinkovic, "What Are Topic Clusters? 6 Ways To Create Content
Clusters" (Surfer SEO Blog, updated 2025-12-11).

## Key takeaways

- Frames topic clusters as the direct product of Google's shift from
  keyword-focused to topic-focused ranking (post-Hummingbird): search
  engines now weigh intent and concept relationships, not isolated
  keyword matches — clusters are the content-structure response to that
  shift.
- Sharpest available definition of **keyword vs. topic**: keywords are
  specific terms placed strategically (titles, URLs, headers, meta,
  body); topics are the broader subject that keywords and subtopics
  roll up into, and should drive overall content strategy rather than
  the reverse.
- 7-step build process (see [[topic-cluster-strategy]] for full
  detail), including specific discovery tactics not covered elsewhere:
  GSC-based clustering of *existing* content via Surfer's Domain Map,
  Google's Related Searches/Autocomplete, and the Keyword Surfer
  extension.
- **Sequential supporting-page linking** (A↔B, B↔C, not just
  supporting→pillar) as a distinct link-juice-flow tactic from the
  standard pillar-hub-and-spoke model.
- Keyword cannibalization defined precisely: multiple pages targeting
  overly similar keywords with identical search intent, causing pages
  to compete internally and diminish each other's rankings — the
  reason cluster pages must have distinct, non-overlapping keyword
  sets.
- Case study: Dino Digital (HR SaaS) achieved a **17x** organic traffic
  increase by building topical authority in one cluster deeply before
  expanding, using Surfer for topical mapping and AI for production.
  Typical build timeline: "a few weeks to a few months" per cluster
  researched and written correctly.
