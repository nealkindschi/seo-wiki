# TOFU Is Back: The 50% Visibility Test for Top-Funnel Content (Siege Media)

**Citation:** Lynch, Taylor. "TOFU Is Back: The 50% Visibility Test for
Top-Funnel Content." Siege Media Research. Last updated 2026-07-09.
https://www.siegemedia.com/research/tofu-content-geo

## Key takeaways

- **A concrete investment-decision framework for TOFU content in the
  LLM era** — a genuinely new decision layer this wiki lacked (existing
  guidance is either mid/bottom-funnel-focused or general topical-
  authority guidance without an explicit go/no-go budget test): a
  topic qualifies for continued TOFU investment only if (1) the brand
  achieves 50%+ visibility across multiple LLM platforms for that
  topic's query, AND (2) the topic carries enough search volume to
  justify it.
- **The underlying mechanism**: LLMs increasingly *name specific
  vendors* when answering category-definition questions, not just
  define the term — e.g. HubSpot appears in 83% of Gemini responses to
  "what is CRM?" This reframes TOFU content's job from "rank for the
  definitional query" to "be the vendor named alongside the
  definition."
- **Qualifying vs. disqualifying topics**: product-connected/category-
  defining questions (answers inherently reference vendors) qualify;
  purely definitional/encyclopedic questions (e.g. "what is
  photosynthesis?") don't, regardless of search volume.
- **Sub-category and problem-framed queries are where challengers can
  win** even against a category leader that dominates the flagship
  term — e.g. "CRM for real estate teams" or "CRM with best mobile
  app" are separate visibility opportunities from "what is CRM."
- **Platform variation is large enough to require multi-platform
  testing**: HubSpot's 83% Gemini visibility for "what is CRM?" doesn't
  carry over to Google's AI Overview for the identical query, where
  Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Oracle NetSuite are named instead.
- **The "re-index method"**: a named, repeatable weekly process —
  sample TOFU topics across multiple LLMs/runs, flag topics at/above
  50% visibility, cross-reference against search volume, prioritize
  investment accordingly, repeat weekly rather than as a one-time
  audit.
