Alex Lindley, "Topic Clusters for SEO" (Semrush Blog, published 2025-10-29).

## Key takeaways

- Topic clusters (one pillar page + reciprocally-linked subpages) build
  topical authority (supporting E-E-A-T), expand keyword *and*
  AI-prompt coverage simultaneously, and improve site structure for
  users, search engines, and LLMs alike.
- 5-step process: choose a core topic (new sites should pick one topic
  and go deep rather than spreading thin) → research topics (volume,
  difficulty, intent, trend stability) → identify pillar/subpages via
  shared-intent keyword clusters → create content (pillar first,
  designed with subpages in mind, reciprocal internal linking, primary
  keyword in title/meta/URL/H1/opening paragraph, write for humans
  first) → measure performance (both Google rank *and* ChatGPT
  appearance, tracked by cluster tag/group, not just individual
  keyword).
- Notably folds AI-citation tracking directly into the standard
  cluster-measurement step — treats "does this cluster appear in
  ChatGPT" as a normal KPI alongside Google position tracking, not a
  separate initiative.
- Worked examples: Healthline's allergy cluster, Petcube's puppy-care
  cluster, Wistia's video-marketing-guide cluster (video-enhanced).
