# Why AI Gets Your Brand Details Wrong—and How to Fix It (seoClarity)

**Citation:** Brosnan, Rebecca. "Why AI Gets Your Brand Details
Wrong—and How to Fix It." seoClarity Blog. Published 2026-06-22.
https://www.seoclarity.net/blog/ai-misrepresentation-and-how-to-fix-it

## Key takeaways

- **A clean four-category root-cause taxonomy for AI misrepresentation**
  that structures the wiki's existing (looser) sentiment/accuracy root-
  cause guidance: missing information (AI fills gaps with assumptions),
  positioning misalignment (AI knows *what* you do but not *who it's
  for* or how you're positioned), unstructured data (facts exist but
  aren't machine-readable), and conflicting external sources (stale
  directories/forums/competitor content create contradiction).
- **Concrete real-world misrepresentation examples**: conflicting phone-
  plan pricing between an AI Overview and a ChatGPT answer; a Home
  Depot location's AI-stated hours (9pm close) not matching its own
  website (10pm close); a luxury brand described by AI as offering less
  "value" than competitors than intended.
- **"Structured data as source-of-truth" framing**: schema markup isn't
  just an extractability aid (the wiki's existing framing throughout
  [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]]) — it's explicitly "a way of
  telling machines which information represents the official version of
  the truth" when sources conflict, a slightly different rationale worth
  keeping alongside the citation-odds framing.
- **A memorable framing line**: "In the era of AI search, mentions get
  you seen. Accuracy earns trust." — a useful complement to the wiki's
  existing citations-vs-mentions distinction
  ([[ai-citation-landscape]]): visibility (mention/citation) and
  accuracy (is the mention *correct*) are separate axes, and this
  source is specifically about the second one.
- **Frames the fix as cross-functional information governance**, not
  just a content/SEO task — explicitly requires SEO, content, PR, and
  data teams working from a shared source of truth, not siloed content
  edits.
