# We rewrote the alt text on Google Store's Pixel Watch 5 page (DEJAN)

DEJAN AI (2026-10-31). dejan.ai/blog/google-store-alt-text/.

**Nature of source**: single-page case study promoting DEJAN's Image
Optimizer. The metric is DEJAN's own (image–text cosine similarity in
Google's multimodal embedding space, with 0.60 displayed as 100%). It
measures alignment, **not** rankings or traffic — no outcome data.

## Key takeaways

1. **Audit findings**: 109 images (63 main content, 46 menu); 47 with no
   alt (45 in main content); menu images labelled with nav text
   ("Phones", "Browse all offers"); placeholders ("Aria Label 2" ×8,
   "Image 1 Desktop/Mobile"); one portrait whose alt described a
   different image. Of 48 real alts, 30 scored under 60%.
2. **Method**: classify images as main content vs. menu/icon/badge;
   then Fill (missing), Quick Fix (one rewrite from image + page +
   current alt), or Deep optimization (10 iterative rewrites, each
   seeing previous versions and scores, keep the best). Brand/model
   names in original alt are preserved; decorative images keep empty alt.
3. **Results (95 images reported)**: average match 30%→84%; main
   content 2%→93%; images that already had alt 58%→78%; no image scored
   lower. 14 placeholder images excluded.
4. **Worked example**: alt described "a macro shot of two bands" but the
   image was a collage of people wearing them — 43%; describing what's
   actually shown jumped to 77%, best 87.4% after 10 iterations.
   Biggest win is describing the actual image, not the product copy.
