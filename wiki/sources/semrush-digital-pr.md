Carlos Silva (contributor: Sydney Go), "Digital PR" (Semrush Blog,
published 2025-02-25).

## Key takeaways

- Digital PR is distinct from plain link building: it targets brand
  visibility/reputation through media coverage, backlinks, *and*
  positive digital-channel mentions together — SEO benefit is one
  output, not the whole goal.
- Five proven tactic types with real examples: data-driven research
  studies (Asana's 13,000-respondent survey → VentureBeat/Forbes
  coverage), creative campaigns (Duolingo TikTok), newsjacking (Surreal
  cereal's AI-trend billboards), expert thought leadership (Qwoted-style
  journalist matching), and product-focused PR (Apple iPad mini 7
  launch).
- Stat worth carrying forward: **61% of journalists want original
  research and reports from PR professionals** (Cision, 2024) — directly
  supports prioritizing original-data assets, which [[growth-memo-why-most-original-data-never-gets-cited]]
  independently found are 3.3x more citation-dense in AI answers too.
- 7-step campaign framework (objectives → audience → competitor
  analysis → newsworthy-content brainstorm → content creation →
  distribution → pitching) — closely parallel in structure to
  [[semrush-digital-pr]]'s sibling content-marketing/SEO-copywriting
  processes, suggesting Semrush's editorial team uses one house
  framework template across guides.
