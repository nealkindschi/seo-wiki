# Ivyforms — The Ultimate Lead Magnet Checklist for Beginners

Data-backed lead-magnet checklist organized by must-have/high-priority/
nice-to-have across strategy, content, delivery, landing page, follow-up,
and tracking — each item paired with a reasoning line and, in many
cases, a supporting stat (form-field count vs. conversion, welcome-email
open rates, opt-in rate targets, etc.).

## Key takeaways
- Format conversion-rate table: quizzes (40.1%) and cheat sheets/checklists
  (~20-35%) outperform ebooks/guides (10-20%) — a concrete tactic missing
  from the more generic LiveAgent checklist ([[liveagent-lead-magnet-checklist]]).
- Form-field count is the single highest-leverage landing-page lever
  cited: dropping from 4 to 3 fields ≈ 50% conversion increase (HubSpot).
- A 5-email, 10-day nurture sequence structure is given with a purpose
  and timing for each email.
- Concrete tracking targets: 20-40% landing-page opt-in rate, <0.5%
  unsubscribe rate.
- All stats are third-party citations (HubSpot, GetResponse, Unbounce,
  Omnisend, CXL, Interact, Wishpond, WPForms, Cropink, Amra and Elma) —
  treat as directional industry benchmarks, not verified primary data.
