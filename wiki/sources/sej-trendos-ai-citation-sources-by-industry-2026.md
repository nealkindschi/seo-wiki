# We Analyzed 107 Million AI Answers: Where Each Industry's Citations Come From (Trendos/SEJ)

**Citation:** Rimolaityte, Gintare (COO & Co-Founder, Trendos). "We
Analyzed 107 Million AI Answers. Here's Where Each Industry's Citations
Actually Come From." Search Engine Journal. Published 2026-08-27.
https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-search-citation-sources-trendos-spa/586289/
(Methodology: sponsored research by Trendos; 107M AI-generated
responses across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/Google AI Overviews;
top-10-citation-source classification into Community & UGC,
Brand/Retail/Owned, and Independent Editorial & Reference. No disclosed
query count, date range, or sampling methodology beyond that.)

## Key takeaways

- **Community & UGC citations hold steady at ~50% across industries**,
  while Brand/Retail vs. Editorial/Reference trade off depending on
  vertical: IT & Solutions Services (51%/2%/47%), Consumer Goods
  (50%/46%/4%), Communication Services (50%/10%/40%).
- **Per-engine personality** (consistent in direction with
  [[brightedge-ai-search-same-brands-different-sources]]'s "engines have
  distinct sourcing personalities" finding in
  [[ai-citation-landscape]]): Google AI Overviews is "the most social"
  (YouTube+Reddit often 4/5 of the top 10); ChatGPT is "the most
  editorial" (Wikipedia/trade media/reference lead); Gemini is "the
  most industry dependent" (editorial for services, brand/retail for
  physical products); Perplexity blends community with directories and
  media.
- **Named B2B/vertical directories** not previously in the wiki: Clutch,
  G2, GoodFirms, Gartner, SourceForge, Slashdot (B2B/software), PCMag,
  TechRadar (consumer tech editorial).
- **Universal-Reddit claim**: Reddit is reported as a top-3 citation
  source across all industries *and all four engines studied*,
  including ChatGPT ("Reddit high on the list").
- **Practical framing by vertical**: B2B software should prioritize
  third-party proof (G2/Clutch/Gartner/trade media) over brand
  homepages; telecom/media should prioritize Wikipedia + earned press
  over more landing pages; e-commerce should treat product/marketplace
  pages as primary "answer copy."
