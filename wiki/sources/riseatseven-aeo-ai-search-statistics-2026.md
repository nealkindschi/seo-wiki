# AEO/AI Search & Citation Statistics 2026 (Rise at Seven)

**Citation:** Rose, Carrie. "AEO/AI Search & Citation Statistics 2026."
Rise at Seven Blog. Published 2026-08-07.
https://riseatseven.com/blog/aeo-ai-search-statistics/
(A stats roundup/compilation synthesizing 22+ studies and platforms,
2024-2026 — not primary research; treat individual stats as only as
reliable as their original source, several of which are already
independently in this wiki.)

## Key takeaways

Since this is a compilation, most stats trace back to sources already
partially represented in the wiki (Ahrefs, Semrush, Pew Research,
BrightEdge, Peec AI, AirOps/Aggarwal's GEO paper). Flagging what's
**genuinely new** vs. **corroborating**:

**Net-new — growth trajectory and scale**:
- ChatGPT weekly active users: 900M (up from 400M in Feb 2025); monthly
  1B (June 2026); ~2.5B daily prompts. Google AI Mode: 1B+ monthly
  users, query volume more than doubling per quarter.
- AI search traffic grew 42.8% (15.6B→27.4B visits) Q1 2025→Q1 2026 vs.
  Google Search's 2.4% growth over the same window — a *growth-rate*
  data point that complements, rather than contradicts,
  [[ai-traffic-scale-vs-hype]]'s "still tiny relative to press
  coverage" *absolute-scale* snapshot (fast growth from a small base is
  compatible with both a huge relative gap and a fast closing rate).
- ChatGPT's share of generative-AI traffic fell from ~76% to ~53%
  (2025→2026) as Gemini climbed past 25% — a genuinely new competitive-
  dynamics data point not previously in the wiki.
- Forecasts: Gartner (Feb 2024) predicts a 25% decline in traditional
  search volume by 2026; Semrush projects AI overtaking traditional
  search by 2028.

**Net-new — citation concentration and volatility**:
- Top 15 domains capture 68% of all consolidated AI citations (a
  power-law concentration figure not previously quantified in the
  wiki).
- **Wikipedia's share of ChatGPT responses crashed from ~55% (August
  2025) to under 20% (September 2025) after a single algorithmic
  adjustment** — a dramatic, dated example of citation volatility at
  the domain level, complementing (not the same data as) the citation-
  *volume* volatility already documented via
  [[seoclarity-chatgpt-citation-decline-analysis]].
- YouTube's share of social citations doubled (18.9%→39.2%, Aug-Dec
  2025) while Reddit's fell (44.2%→20.3%) over the same window — a
  fast, sizable reshuffling within the UGC citation layer.
- Government sites: 6% citation rate in AI search vs. 2% in traditional
  search — net-new.
- Perplexity concentrates 46.7% of its top-10 citation sources on
  Reddit alone — sharpens the existing "Perplexity/Gemini favor Reddit"
  pattern in [[ai-citation-landscape]] with a concentration number.
- Freshness: 83% of commercial-query AI citations come from content
  updated within 12 months, 60%+ within 6 months; ~65% of AI bot
  crawls target content under 1 year old — corroborates and sharpens
  existing freshness guidance in [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]].

**Net-new — zero-click/CTR trajectory**:
- CTR suppression at organic position 1 worsened from 34.5% (Ahrefs,
  April 2025) to 58% (Ahrefs, Dec 2025) — **this is the missing
  pre-reversal trajectory data point** for
  [[aio-ctr-impact]]'s "2025 decline, then a 2026 reversal" section:
  suppression got progressively worse through 2025 (April→December),
  then reversed starting in early 2026 (per that page's existing Seer
  Interactive data, Dec 2025 1.3%→Feb 2026 2.4% cited-brand CTR). The
  two datasets are sequential and consistent, not measuring the same
  thing at the same time.
- Google AI Mode zero-click rate: 93% (vs. ~83% for AI Overview
  presence generally, and 68% for all Google searches) — AI Mode is
  the most click-suppressive surface measured, a sharper number than
  previously in the wiki.
- Only 1% of visits click a source link inside an AI summary — a
  stark, previously-unquantified number for how rarely a citation
  converts to a click even when present.

**Net-new — marketer adoption gap**:
- 70% of marketers believe AEO will impact strategy within 1-3 years,
  but only 20% are currently implementing it (Acquia/Researchscape,
  n=500+) — a **different reading than** [[airops-geo-strategy-playbook]]'s
  Branch 2026 stat (98% of enterprise marketing leaders already
  optimizing or planning to within 12 months). Flagged as a genuine
  survey-methodology/population discrepancy (different survey firms,
  likely different respondent seniority/company-size mix) rather than
  a resolved contradiction — worth citing both with the caveat that
  "adoption rate" varies wildly by how the question is asked and who's
  asked.

**Corroborating (already in wiki, same underlying study)**:
- Quotation addition +37%, statistics addition +22%, up to 40% max
  visibility lift — this is the same Aggarwal et al. GEO paper already
  cited throughout [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]]'s Tier 1
  tactics; no new numbers.
- Reddit/YouTube/Wikipedia as heavily-cited domains, freshness as a
  citation factor, AI citation halo effect on organic clicks — same
  direction as existing [[ai-citation-landscape]] and [[aio-ctr-impact]]
  findings.

## Relationship to existing wiki claims

No direct conflicts. One genuine open discrepancy flagged (marketer
adoption rate, 20% vs. 98%, likely a survey-methodology difference, not
logged as a resolved conflict since neither source's methodology is
transparent enough in this wiki to adjudicate). Everything else either
extends existing pages with new numbers/trajectory data or corroborates
findings already sourced elsewhere.
