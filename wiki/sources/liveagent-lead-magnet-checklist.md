# LiveAgent — Lead Magnet Checklist

Vendor checklist (LiveAgent, a help-desk/live-chat product) covering the
full lead-magnet lifecycle: planning (audience, value prop, resourcing),
content creation (title, keywords, format, CTA, mobile, proofreading),
and post-launch measurement (sign-up rate, form completion, ROI,
download rate, engagement, open/CTR, unsubscribe rate, lead quality,
time-to-convert).

## Key takeaways
- Broad, generic 22-item checklist spanning strategy → content → distribution
  → measurement — a planning-stage complement to [[ivyforms-lead-magnet-checklist]]'s
  more tactical, benchmarked checklist.
- One item ("Add Chat Button") is a self-serving vendor plug for live
  chat widgets rather than a general best practice — included in
  [[lead-magnet-checklist]] with that caveat noted, not as a universal
  requirement.
- No conversion-rate benchmarks or stats given — purely a process
  checklist, unlike Ivyforms' data-backed version.
