# The Content That Predicts B2B AI Traffic Most: Versus Pages (Siege Media)

**Citation:** Hudgens, Ross. "The Content That Predicts B2B AI Traffic
Most: Versus Pages." Siege Media Research. Last updated 2026-06-01.
https://www.siegemedia.com/research/versus-pages
(116 B2B GA4 properties, trailing 90 days ending 2026-05-24, 739,492 AI
search sessions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot,
Meta AI. Spearman rank correlation, primary; Pearson on log-transformed
values, secondary.)

## Key takeaways

- **Versus ("X vs Y") pages are the single strongest content-type
  predictor of B2B AI search traffic measured anywhere in this
  wiki**: Spearman correlation of 0.65, double the next-best content
  type (alternatives/"best X software" pages), which compete directly
  with G2/Capterra/affiliate sites for the same citation instead of
  matching the AI's exact query-intent shape.
- **A concrete, staged volume curve**: 1-5 comparison pages = baseline;
  6-20 pages = +350% median AI sessions; 21+ pages = +900% vs. the 1-5
  group, with diminishing but still-positive returns beyond 20.
- **Content explains only ~28% of AI-traffic variance** — the remaining
  ~72% is brand authority, category fit, and citation-graph density.
  One in five top-20 sites had fewer than five transactional pages,
  meaning brand authority alone can substitute for content volume at
  the very top of the distribution.
- **Pricing pages are a correlation artifact, not an independent
  driver** — a raw 21x difference vanished once controlling for overall
  content investment (91% of pricing-page sites also had versus pages;
  only 37% of sites hiding pricing did).
- **International/multi-locale comparison programs underperform** their
  page count would predict, due to smaller regional search volume and
  lower international AI adoption — though alternatives content
  specifically shows 20-55pp higher engagement internationally, a
  worthwhile local exception to test.
- **A prompt-tracking idea**: create a dedicated "competitive
  comparison" prompt category to track sentiment shifts between your
  brand and named competitors over time.
