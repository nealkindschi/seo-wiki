# What Best "X" Posts Actually Deliver + Why Investment Matters (Siege Media)

**Citation:** Mankle, Marianna. "What Best 'X' Posts Actually Deliver +
Why Investment Matters." Siege Media Research. Last updated 2026-06-30.
https://www.siegemedia.com/research/best-x-pages
(101 "best X" B2B pages, 12 sites, 12 months of GA4 data, March
2025-Feb 2026.)

## Key takeaways

- **Classic web-analytics benchmarks for BOFU listicle content**, a
  metric layer the wiki previously lacked (existing "best X" coverage
  in [[listicles-in-ai-search]] and [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]]
  is all AI-citation-focused, not GA4 engagement-focused): median
  engagement rate 61.1%, median session duration 113 seconds, median
  pages/session 1.0 (vs. 2.73 for the top-performing site category) —
  a large, addressable architecture gap for most sites.
- **Session duration is the primary quality signal**, driven
  specifically by deep comparison tables, transparent pricing, and
  same-page answers to follow-up questions — a GA4-measurable version
  of what [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]] already recommends for
  citation purposes (structure, quotable facts), now with a
  conversion-metric rationale layered on top.
- **"Best for" callouts are the highest-weight decision column** —
  readers scroll back to them more than any other table element.
- **Audience-content mismatch, not content quality alone, drives
  bounce**: mismatched traffic sources see 70%+ bounce regardless of
  content quality — a reminder that structural/citation optimization
  doesn't fix a fundamentally wrong-audience page.
- **In-body linking from each listicle entry to the matching
  product/service page** is recommended as a distinct architectural
  fix for low pages-per-session, treating each entry as "a mini
  conversion funnel."

## Relationship to existing wiki claims

- **Complements, doesn't overlap with**, the AI-citation-focused best-X/
  listicle guidance already in [[listicles-in-ai-search]] and
  [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]]'s "Vendor evaluation readiness"
  and "Target listicle rank" sections — this source is about
  traditional GA4 engagement/conversion outcomes of BOFU listicles,
  not AI citation odds. The two are complementary metrics for the same
  content type (citation-optimize *and* engagement-optimize the same
  page), not competing claims.
- **No conflicts.**
