# Brand Visibility Is the North Star for AI Search (AirOps)

**Citation:** Josh Spilker. "Brand Visibility Is the North Star for AI
Search." AirOps. Published 2025-07-31.
https://www.airops.com/report/north-star-metric-ai-search

## Key takeaways

- **Proposes a single composite "Brand Visibility Score"** predating
  [[airops-geo-strategy-playbook]]'s later five-metric framework:
  Answers mentioning your brand ÷ Total answers for your category,
  built from Citation Rate, Sentiment Score, and Share of Voice.
- **Gives concrete formulas** the wiki didn't previously have:
  - Sentiment Score = (Positive mentions + 0.5 × Neutral mentions) ÷
    Total mentions.
  - Brand Visibility Score = brand-mentioning answers ÷ total
    category answers.
- **"AI doesn't click. It answers."** — framing for why click/pageview/
  CTR/MQL metrics fail in an AI-answer-dominated search landscape.
- **Five-pillar execution model**: Strategy (set visibility agenda) →
  Metrics (pipeline-driving indicators) → Execution (audit/plan/build
  phases for agent-extractable content) → Team Structure (Content
  Engineer / Content Strategist / Growth Engineer roles) → Growth
  (visibility-triggered workflows: create/refresh/outreach/community).
- **AI Overview adoption figures**: <5% of Google queries (Jan 2025) →
  25% (Jul 2025) → a claimed "current" 50% of all U.S. Google queries,
  with ~60% of U.S. queries "influenced by AI."
- **Named case-study companies** (no per-company detail extracted):
  Angi (79%-better-converting longtail content, previously known from
  [[airops-geo-strategy-playbook]]), Webflow, Ramp, Descript, Carta,
  Gong.

## Relationship to existing wiki claims

- **Superseded-with-reasoning by** [[airops-geo-strategy-playbook]]
  (2026-07-21, the same publisher, ~1 year later): that report splits
  this page's single "Brand Visibility Score" composite into five
  separately-tracked weekly metrics (citation rate, mention rate, share
  of voice, sentiment, source attribution) rather than one blended
  score — see [[generative-engine-optimization]]'s measurement-framework
  section, now updated to note the evolution. This page's concrete
  Sentiment Score and Brand Visibility Score *formulas* are still net-new
  and worth keeping, since the later report doesn't specify formulas.
- **Agrees/extends** [[genai-search-90-day-sprint]] with an independent
  "90-day roadmap" framing (though this source gives no phase-level
  detail, unlike the Similarweb-sourced sprint already in the wiki) and
  a team-structure/role model (Content Engineer, Content Strategist,
  Growth Engineer) that's genuinely new to the wiki.
- **Conflicts** with [[semrush-ai-overviews-study-2025]] on AI Overview
  prevalence: this source's "current state: 50% of all U.S. Google
  queries include AI-generated summaries" (as of ~July 2025) is far
  above Semrush's 10M+-keyword full-year-2025 study, which found
  prevalence peaking at 24.61% in July 2025 and *settling back down* to
  15.69% by November 2025 — the same July-2025 month this source's own
  "25%" figure roughly matches, but the claimed "current"/50% figure
  does not. Logged as a Conflicting Evidence addition to
  [[aio-ctr-impact]] (see below) rather than silently preferring either
  number — plausible explanations include a broader "influenced by AI"
  definition (which the source separately states as ~60%) being
  conflated with strict AI Overview presence, or a less rigorous
  estimation methodology than Semrush/Datos' keyword-panel tracking.
