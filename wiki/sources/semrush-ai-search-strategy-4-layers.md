Alex Lindley (contributor Faizan Ali), "AI search strategy: 4 layers of
brand visibility" (Semrush Blog, 2026-10-23). Raw copy is a fetch-tool
paraphrase. Vendor content (promotes Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit and
Prompt Tracking); study figures are cited without full methodology here.

## Key takeaways

- Premise: AI answers pass through retrieval, evaluation, synthesis, and
  citation; a gap at any step excludes or distorts a brand, so diagnose
  the layer before choosing a fix. Four layers: **discoverability,
  clarity, authority, trust**. Triage model, not confirmed ranking
  factors.
- Discoverability: crawlable/indexable/text-readable pages, AI crawlers
  not blocked by robots.txt or CDN/WAF rules (check 403/429 in logs).
  Google states AIO/AI Mode need no special technical requirements or
  schema; ChatGPT Search relies on OAI-SearchBot.
- Clarity: accessible content can still be described wrongly. Keep a
  verified source of truth and audit AI descriptions against it. Semrush
  example: AI called it a traditional SEO tool; the fix was updating
  third-party pages LLMs cite, not publishing more own-site content.
- Authority is about **information gain** (proprietary data, firsthand
  tests, original frameworks). Semrush content study: cited pages beat
  top-20 ranking pages on clear summarization (+32.83%), E-E-A-T signals
  (+30.64%), Q&A formatting (+25.45%), section structure (+22.91%), and
  visible structured data such as lists/tables (+21.60%, not schema).
- Trust: independent corroboration (reviews, publisher coverage);
  contradictions surface as caveats and hedged recommendations.
- Data points: Reddit's share of ChatGPT responses fell from ~60% (early
  Aug 2025) to ~10% (mid-Sept 2025), stabilizing alongside Wikipedia as
  the top-2 cited domains. Across 3,981 brand appearances (ChatGPT, AIO,
  Gemini, AI Mode), 62% were citations without the brand being named;
  ChatGPT cited brands in 87% of appearances but named them in 20.7%.
- Measure presence, representation, source influence, and business
  outcomes separately, with 20-25 buyer-decision prompts as a baseline.
- Six mistakes include treating schema or llms.txt as citation switches
  (Google says it doesn't use AI-specific files; no published evidence
  they raise citations) and manufacturing third-party consensus.
- "Answer-ready" vs. "agent-ready" distinction; agent-ready adds
  navigable, ARIA-labeled sites. Closing filter: a tactic is strategic
  only if it still helps when the platform changes.
