# Why Your Content Isn't Getting Cited: A GEO Diagnostic Checklist (Lumar)

Lumar blog post proposing a six-part diagnostic checklist for
triaging *why* a page isn't getting cited by generative engines,
before jumping to optimization tactics. No original data, studies, or
statistics — a framework piece, leaning on
[[google-ai-optimization-guide]] for its indexing/retrieval claims.

## Key takeaways

1. **Diagnose before optimizing.** Ranking position alone doesn't tell
   you which of several distinct failure modes is in play (eligibility,
   relevance, chunkability, authority) — applying a random tactic
   without diagnosis risks fixing the wrong layer.
2. **Six diagnostic checks**, roughly ordered as a funnel:
   - Candidate page eligibility (indexed, focused, title matches intent)
   - Clear "aboutness" signals (core concept introduced early,
     opening content stands alone)
   - Chunk retrievability (focused, coherent sections)
   - Standalone passage context (pronouns/references resolvable without
     surrounding text, entities/units/constraints included)
   - Authority constraints (functions as "a filter, not a score" —
     matters more for sensitive/safety-critical queries)
   - Problem-solution alignment (verify a fix targets the actual
     diagnosed failure, not a generic "AI-friendly" change)
3. Frames authority as binary-ish gating rather than a ranking signal —
   consistent with this wiki's existing framing in
   [[generative-engine-optimization]].
