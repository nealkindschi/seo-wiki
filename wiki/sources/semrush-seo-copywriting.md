Carlos Silva, "SEO Copywriting: 10 Tips to Boost Your Content
Performance" (Semrush Blog, published 2023-01-31).

## Key takeaways

- SEO copywriting = writing that's simultaneously optimized for search
  algorithms and genuinely engaging for human readers — the article's
  10 tips split roughly into keyword/intent research (tips 1-4),
  on-page optimization (tips 6, 10), and pure readability/UX craft
  (tips 7-9).
- Sharpest tactical addition beyond generic on-page advice: use
  "People Also Ask" and question-mining tools (AlsoAsked,
  AnswerThePublic, Quora, Topic Research) as a *content-idea* source
  distinct from keyword-volume research — answers a different question
  ("what do people want explained") than keyword tools do ("what do
  people type").
- Readability craft: short paragraphs leading with the main idea
  (inverted pyramid / BLUF — "bottom line up front"), simple vocabulary,
  explicit jargon avoidance — directly reusable as house style guidance
  for any content produced from this wiki's own playbooks.
- Table-of-contents recommendation doubles as a self-check: if you can't
  write a clean, specific TOC for a draft, the content structure likely
  needs work before publishing.
