# Digital Chakra — How to Optimise PDF Files for SEO

11-point PDF-specific SEO checklist (unique content, title/description
metadata, filename, anchor text, internal linking, real header tags,
URL structure, alt text, file-size compression, standard fonts, mobile
formatting), plus technical notes on PDF indexability, ranking behavior
vs. HTML, and download tracking.

## Key takeaways
- Core framing claim: "A Web document is more likely to rank higher
  than a PDF in 9 out of 10 cases" — PDFs should be treated as a
  distribution/lead-magnet format, not a primary ranking vehicle;
  duplicate PDF+HTML content typically loses to the HTML version.
- Technical constraints not covered elsewhere in this wiki: images
  inside PDFs aren't indexed by Google, nofollow can't be applied within
  a PDF, and encrypted/password-protected/non-static-HTML PDFs aren't
  indexed at all.
- Standard GA doesn't track PDF downloads — needs GTM or a plugin.
- Practical overlap with [[image-seo-checklist]] (alt text, compression)
  and [[technical-seo-audit-checklist]] (noindex control via
  X-Robots-Tag) — cross-linked rather than duplicated.
