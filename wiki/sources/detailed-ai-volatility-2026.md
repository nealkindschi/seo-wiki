# How Volatile Are AI Responses? (Detailed)

**Citation:** Allsopp, Glen. "How Volatile Are AI Responses?" Detailed,
2026-09-23. https://detailed.com/ai-volatility/

28 days of daily checks on 1,300+ commercial prompts in ChatGPT and
Google AI Overviews (70,000+ responses). Prompts were keyword-derived
and rephrased conversationally; single-turn only (no follow-ups).

## Key takeaways

- **Exact answers almost never repeat.** Two random days returned the
  identical brand list 0.3% (ChatGPT) / 1.1% (AIO) of the time; 41% of
  ChatGPT prompts and 30% of AIO prompts *never* repeated a brand set.
- **But the leader persists.** The most-named brand per prompt was
  present on 82% (ChatGPT) / 89% (AIO) of days; chosen from days 1–14
  alone, it still held 79% / 85% presence on days 15–28.
- **Stable core, churning tail.** Brands present on ≥80% of days changed
  ~13% / ~11% day to day; everything else churned 78% / 65%. Only ~half
  of ChatGPT prompts had any core brand (usually one or two).
- **Lead margins are thin.** #1 vs #2 typically differ by ~2 days out of
  28; the leader changed between fortnights on ~half of prompts.
- **Position is noisier than presence.** The usual leader was named
  *first* on 50% / 60% of days vs. present on 67% / 78%. The most-cited
  domain was present 82% / 89% but cited first only 43% / 28%.
- **URLs churn, content types don't.** ~25% (ChatGPT) vs ~50% (AIO) of
  cited pages were re-cited next day; articles stayed ~60% of citations
  on both.
- **AIO video citations fell** from ~14% (week 1) to ~8% (week 4).
- **Recommendation:** stop exact-match tracking; measure presence and
  share over a window.

## Caveats

Single practitioner study; no published statistical tests in the
fetched text. Commercial prompts only, two engines, one 28-day window
(dates not stated beyond publication). "Identical" test is strict by the
author's own admission. Raw file is a fetch-tool paraphrase.
