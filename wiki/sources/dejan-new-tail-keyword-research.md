# Advanced New Tail Keyword Research (DEJAN)

Petrovic, D. / DEJAN AI. dejan.ai/blog/newtail/. Originally a **2016**
post (the survey method, Google Surveys, and the "15% of queries are
new" quote date from then); republished 2026-07-27 with a new "New tail
in AI search" section.

**Nature of source**: single-product case study (n=100 survey), plus
2026 practitioner commentary.

## Key takeaways

1. **"New tail" queries** are what people type when they don't know a
   product's name — early-research descriptions by location,
   application, properties, or analogy ("moss-like grass"). Not
   necessarily long.
2. **Method**: show ~100 people a product photo (image + open-ended
   mode, ~$10 survey) and ask what they'd search. For Zoysia tenuifolia
   grass: 68 terms, only 24 on the top-ranking page (35% match), 44
   missed ("lumpy", "mossy", "bumpy", "fuzzy"…).
3. The ranking page focused on "no mow" — an **internal information
   bubble** of what the owner assumes customers care about.
4. **Use**: tag/cluster responses (spreadsheet + COUNTIFS), categorize
   as content-improving / irrelevant / new-page ideas, prioritise by
   volume, value, conversion, difficulty. Add a natural-language
   visual-properties section, improve alt text, consider a "types of"
   gallery page. Don't stuff keywords. Demographic splits can tailor copy.
5. **2026 addition**: fanout sub-queries written by the assistant never
   reach Search Console, and implicit queries (from photos, screenshots,
   open pages) have no typed wording at all. A page that names
   attributes in natural language yields grounding snippets that carry
   those attributes. **New step**: survey the model too — give it the
   photo and question repeatedly; where model and human vocabularies
   differ, cover both.
