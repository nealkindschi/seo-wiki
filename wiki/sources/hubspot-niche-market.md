Meredith Hart, "Niche Markets: Examples, Benefits, Expert Insight, and
How to Find Yours" (HubSpot Blog, last updated 2026-03-18). Raw copy is a
fetch-tool paraphrase, not verbatim. Business-strategy rather than SEO
content; beginner level.

## Key takeaways

- Six steps to find a niche: reflect on passions/skills, identify
  customer problems (personas, talk to prospects, "space within the
  space"), research competition, define the niche and assess
  profitability, test before scaling, articulate a value proposition.
- Tools named: Exploding Topics, Google Trends, AnswerThePublic,
  Ubersuggest, Keyword Planner, ClickBank, Amazon, G2, PRICEFY.IO.
- Test cheaply: landing page, trials/free samples, modest ads,
  crowdfunding; refine rather than abandon on weak results.
- Expert quotes lean passion-first ("let your niche find you"), which
  contrasts with [[surnex-finding-a-niche]]'s demand-first stance; see
  the Conflicting Evidence section on [[niche-selection-and-validation]].
- No AI-search or SEO-specific data; examples are brands (Drybar,
  SoulCycle, Ties.com) rather than search-driven sites.
