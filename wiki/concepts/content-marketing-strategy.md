# Content Marketing Strategy

Content marketing is the umbrella discipline this wiki's more tactical
content playbooks sit under: research/develop/align content for a
target audience, then promote and distribute it. This page is
intentionally thin — it exists to connect the tactical playbooks rather
than duplicate their content.

## How the pieces fit together

- **Discover what to write about**: [[keyword-research]] (demand
  discovery) and [[seo-competitive-analysis]] (competitive-gap
  discovery) both feed the content plan.
- **Decide the content's structural role**: [[topic-cluster-strategy]]
  (pillar/subpage/target-page structure) and
  [[keyword-mapping-and-cannibalization]] (one keyword, one page).
- **Write it**: [[seo-copywriting]] (on-page craft and structure).
- **Earn it visibility beyond ranking**: [[digital-pr-strategy]] and
  [[broken-link-building]] (link/mention earning),
  [[brand-entity-seo-strategy]] (machine-legible brand signals).
- **Optimize for AI-era discovery specifically**:
  [[generative-engine-optimization]] and
  [[geo-content-optimization-tactics]].
- **Measure and report**: [[seo-reporting]].
- **Convert readers to leads**: [[lead-magnet-checklist]] (opt-in offer
  and landing-page conversion mechanics).

Semrush Academy's current content-marketing curriculum
