Why / when to use this: use when the goal is brand visibility and
reputation, not just backlinks — digital PR earns links as a byproduct
of media coverage, alongside referral traffic and third-party
credibility that pure link-building tactics ([[broken-link-building]])
don't provide. Complements [[brand-entity-seo-strategy]] (digital PR
coverage is one of the "corroborating platforms" that strengthens
brand-entity signals) and [[content-marketing-strategy]] (PR-worthy
content is often the same original-research/data assets that drive
organic content performance).

## What it is

Digital PR builds brand visibility and reputation through earned media
coverage, high-quality backlinks, and positive mentions across digital
channels — distinct from plain link building because the goal set is
broader (reputation + referral traffic + authority, not backlinks
alone).

## Five tactic types (with examples)

1. **Data-driven research studies** — original research earns coverage
   because it gives journalists something new to report. Example:
   Asana's 2024 State of Work Innovation survey (13,000+ respondents,
   6 countries) earned VentureBeat and Forbes coverage.
2. **Creative campaigns** — memorable, platform-native content.
   Example: Duolingo's chaotic-mascot TikTok campaign reached 15M+
   followers.
3. **Newsjacking** — timely commentary on a live trend. Example:
   Surreal cereal's AI-copywriting-trend billboards generated 1,400+
   LinkedIn likes.
4. **Expert thought leadership** — connecting subject-matter experts
   with journalists seeking commentary (e.g. via services like Qwoted).
5. **Product-focused PR** — newsworthy launches. Example: Apple's iPad
   mini 7 launch generated coverage across outlets like ZDNet.

## 7-Step campaign framework

1. **Set clear objectives** — measurable goals, specific tactics
   assigned, success metrics, a timeline (e.g. a 6-month brand-awareness
   campaign).
2. **Understand your audience** — identify target publications, consult
   sales teams on where customers actually congregate, use
   audience-analysis tools for demographic/behavioral data.
3. **Analyze competition** — study competitors' successful coverage
   patterns; run backlink gap analysis to find publications linking
   competitors but not you.
4. **Brainstorm newsworthy content** — original data, industry trends,
   audience pain points. **61% of journalists want original research
   and reports from PR professionals** (Cision, 2024) — prioritize
   original-data assets over rehashed commentary.
5. **Create unique content** — compelling narrative structure, visuals/
   data visualizations, an explained methodology, and an accessible
   press-kit package.
6. **Develop distribution strategy** — coordinate owned (site, social),
   earned (news outlets), and paid (ads, influencers) channels; target
   specific journalists already covering the topic.
7. **Write compelling pitches** — a strong subject line, the
   newsworthiness summarized in three sentences, specific stats
   included, a clear call to action.

## Monitoring & measurement

- Google Alerts for brand-mention notifications.
- Sentiment analysis via social-listening/monitoring tools.
- Track mention volume, reach, and a "presence score."
- Identify the most influential sites linking to you, to prioritize
  future outreach targets.

## Checklist

- [ ] Campaign objective and success metric defined before outreach
      starts.
- [ ] Target publication/journalist list built from audience research
      and competitor backlink-gap analysis.
- [ ] Content asset is original (data, research, or genuinely novel
      angle) — not a rehash.
- [ ] Distribution plan spans owned + earned + paid, not earned alone.
- [ ] Pitch has a specific subject line, 3-sentence newsworthiness
      summary, concrete stats, and a clear CTA.
- [ ] Mention/sentiment monitoring set up post-launch.
