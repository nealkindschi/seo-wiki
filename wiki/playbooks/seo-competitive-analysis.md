Why / when to use this: run periodically (e.g. quarterly) or when
entering a new topic/product area, to find keyword, content, backlink,
and AI-visibility gaps relative to whoever's actually winning that
space — including competitors outside your literal industry.
Complements [[seo-reporting]] (competitive data is a standard report
input) and [[keyword-research]] (gap analysis here feeds directly into
that playbook's discovery methods).

## Direct vs. organic competitors

- **Direct competitors**: operate in the same industry as you.
- **Organic competitors**: rank for the same keywords despite operating
  in a different industry. Worked example: a health/fitness publisher
  treats an apparel brand as an organic competitor because they compete
  for the same fitness-content search terms, even though they don't
  compete commercially. Don't limit competitive analysis to your literal
  industry peer list.

## 8-Step process

1. **Identify competitors.** Search 3-5 core keywords across Google,
   YouTube, Reddit, and AI platforms; pull a tool's "main organic
   competitors" report; sort into direct vs. organic.
2. **Analyze traffic.** Organic and branded traffic trends over time,
   top pages by visitor volume, multiple traffic sources (AI referrals,
   social, email, display), and whether traffic shifts correlate with
   known algorithm updates.
3. **Analyze keyword footprints.** Keyword gap analysis for terms
   competitors rank for that you don't; filter by commercial/
   transactional intent and top-10 position; segment findings into
   topic clusters (see [[topic-cluster-strategy]]).
4. **Check AI visibility.** Use an AI-visibility tool to compare
   cross-platform presence; identify prompts where competitors appear
   and you don't, filtered to business-relevant prompts only — the
   AI-search equivalent of step 3's keyword gap analysis.
5. **Reverse-engineer winning pages.** For competitor pages ranking
   top-10 and driving real traffic, evaluate: angle, audience
   targeting, H2 structure, content format, title tags, and authority
   signals (original research, named frameworks, credibility markers).
   Reusable as a standing competitor-teardown template.
6. **Analyze backlinks.** Authority Score/Domain Rating and
   referring-domain trends; backlink gap analysis for sites linking
   competitors but not you; note which content *types* attract the most
   links.
7. **Explore off-site presence.** Social posting frequency/engagement;
   Reddit/community `site:` searches for user sentiment, praised
   benefits, and pain points — extends analysis beyond owned-site SEO
   into brand perception (overlaps [[brand-entity-seo-strategy]]'s
   branded-query analysis).
8. **Review local SEO** (if applicable). Google Business Profile
   comparison, review sentiment/complaint handling, local search
   visibility, and how AI platforms present local competitors.

## Turning findings into action

Document results in a standing, recurring-monitoring format (not a
one-off deck) — traffic, backlinks, paid search, and social should all
be tracked on the same cadence as competitors evolve.

## Checklist

- [ ] Competitor list includes organic (cross-industry) competitors,
      not just direct-industry peers.
- [ ] Keyword gap analysis run and filtered to commercial/transactional
      intent.
- [ ] AI-visibility gap analysis run separately from traditional
      keyword gap analysis.
- [ ] At least 2-3 top-performing competitor pages reverse-engineered
      against the angle/structure/authority checklist.
- [ ] Backlink gap analysis identifies specific outreach targets, not
      just aggregate authority scores.
- [ ] Off-site sentiment (social, Reddit/community) reviewed, not just
      on-site metrics.
- [ ] Findings filed into a recurring-monitoring format, not a one-time
      report.
