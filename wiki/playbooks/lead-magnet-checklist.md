# Lead Magnet Checklist

Why / when to use this: a lead magnet is the free-value offer (checklist,
template, ebook, mini-course, quiz) traded for an email address or other
contact info. Use this checklist when planning, building, or auditing
one — it complements [[content-marketing-strategy]] (the content itself)
and [[seo-copywriting]] (on-page writing craft) but is scoped specifically
to opt-in conversion mechanics.

## 1. Strategy (must-have)
- Solves **one** specific problem — narrow focus converts better than a
  broad guide.
- Targets a clearly defined audience — relevance beats reach.
- Connects logically to a paid offer — the lead needs an obvious next
  step toward buying, or the sequence goes nowhere.

## 2. Content
- Specific, benefit-driven title (not vague) — must-have.
- Delivers one quick, tangible win / instant gratification — must-have.
- Demonstrates expertise or POV — high priority, positions you as an
  authority before any sales conversation.
- Keep it short: "a few pages" is enough; 58.6% of marketers report
  higher conversion from short-form vs. long-form lead magnets
  (GetResponse, via [[ivyforms-lead-magnet-checklist]]).
- Visual breaks (headings, lists, bolded text), varied media, and a
  simple visual hierarchy — from [[liveagent-lead-magnet-checklist]].

## 3. Format — pick by conversion rate vs. effort

| Format | Conversion Rate | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| PDF Checklist/Template | 20-35% | Low |
| Swipe File/Cheat Sheet | 25-34% | Low |
| Short Video Tutorial | 20-30% | Medium |
| Mini Email Course | 15-25% | Medium |
| Ebook/Guide | 10-20% | High |
| Quiz | 40.1% avg | Medium |

(Interact's 40.1% quiz figure is drawn from 80M+ leads and is the
single highest-converting format in either source —
[[ivyforms-lead-magnet-checklist]].)

If delivering as a PDF specifically, see [[pdf-seo-optimization]] for
file-level optimization (this is about the offer/opt-in mechanics, not
the file itself).

## 4. Delivery
- Instantly accessible after opt-in — delays kill momentum.
- Mobile-optimized — most downloads happen on phones (82% of landing
  page visitors are mobile, per Unbounce).
- Printable or editable format available — increases perceived value.

## 5. Landing page
- Dedicated page, **no navigation bar** — removes distractions.
- **3 form fields or fewer.** Reducing from 4 to 3 fields is worth
  roughly a 50% conversion increase (HubSpot); ideal is name+email or
  email only. This is the single highest-leverage lever in the whole
  checklist.
- Avoid asking for phone number — 37% of people abandon forms that
  request one (WPForms).
- One CTA only: multiple CTAs on a landing page can cut conversions by
  up to 266% (Wishpond).
- Action-oriented CTA copy ("Get my checklist") outperforms generic
  copy ("Submit").
- Inline field validation reduces form errors 22% and speeds completion
  42% (CXL).
- Benchmarks: median landing-page conversion is 6.6% across 41,000
  pages (Unbounce 2024); dedicated lead-gen pages average 9-12%, top
  performers 20-30%; a good lead-magnet-specific opt-in rate target is
  **20-40%**.

## 6. Follow-up sequence
Send the delivery email immediately — first-email open rates run
68-80%. Then run a 4-7 email nurture sequence:

| Email | Purpose | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deliver magnet, set expectations | Immediate |
| 2 | Quick win or personal story | Day 2 |
| 3 | Address top myth or mistake | Day 4 |
| 4 | Social proof / case study | Day 7 |
| 5 | Soft pitch / next-step CTA | Day 10 |

Three welcome emails generate 90% more orders than a single message
(Omnisend 2023); automated emails account for 41% of orders from just
2% of total sends (Omnisend) — the sequence, not the single delivery
email, is where the ROI is.

## 7. Tracking
- Landing page opt-in rate — target 20-40%.
- Unsubscribe rate — target below 0.5%.
- Sign-up rate, form completion rate, download rate, engagement, email
  open/CTR, lead quality, time-to-convert (fuller metric list from
  [[liveagent-lead-magnet-checklist]]).
- A/B test headline or CTA — only 17% of marketers actually do this,
  but testers see a 37% average improvement (Cropink). Low effort, high
  neglect, worth prioritizing.

## 8. Promotion channels
- Organic: blog content upgrades, social pinned posts, homepage
  banners, exit-intent popups, sidebar widgets.
- Paid: Facebook/Instagram lead ads, Google Ads on problem-based
  keywords, newsletter swaps, podcast guest appearances.
- Multi-channel promotion reduces cost-per-lead ~31% vs. single-channel
  (Amra and Elma 2025).

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Offer too long or generic instead of solving one specific problem.
- Excessive form fields.
- No follow-up sequence after initial delivery.
- Lead magnet topic disconnected from the paid offering it should lead
  toward.
- Ignoring mobile optimization.
- Security-anxiety triggers on the form (29% cite security concerns as
  the top reason for abandonment) and general usability friction (67%
  abandon on usability issues, WPForms) — keep the form short and low-friction.
