# The Ultimate Lead Magnet Checklist for Beginners

Source: https://ivyforms.com/blog/lead-magnet-checklist/

## Quick Lead Magnet Checklist

### Strategy
- Solves one specific problem (Must-have) — narrow focus converts better than broad guides
- Targets clearly defined audience (Must-have) — relevance beats reach; wrong audience = no conversions
- Connects logically to paid offer (Must-have) — leads need clear next step toward buying

### Content
- Delivers one quick, tangible win (Must-have) — instant gratification builds trust faster
- Has specific, benefit-driven title (Must-have) — vague titles don't get clicked
- Demonstrates expertise or POV (High priority) — positions you as authority before sales conversation

### Delivery
- Instantly accessible after opt-in (Must-have) — delays kill momentum
- Optimized for mobile (Must-have) — most downloads happen on phones
- Available in printable or editable format (High priority) — increases perceived value

### Landing Page
- Dedicated page with no navigation bar (Must-have) — removes distractions
- Form has 3 fields or fewer (Must-have) — fewer fields = up to 50% higher conversion
- CTA button uses action-oriented text (Must-have) — "Get my checklist" outperforms "Submit"

### Follow-Up
- Delivery email sent immediately (Must-have) — first email open rates hit 68-80%
- Nurture sequence of 4-7 emails (Must-have) — moves leads from interested to ready-to-buy
- Sequence ends with clear soft pitch (High priority) — without next step, leads go cold

### Tracking
- Landing page opt-in rate monitored (High priority) — target 20-40%
- Unsubscribe rate tracked (High priority) — target below 0.5%
- A/B test run on headline or CTA (Nice to have) — small changes can lift conversions 10-30%

## Follow-Up Email Sequence Structure
| Email | Purpose | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deliver magnet, set expectations | Immediate |
| 2 | Share quick win or personal story | Day 2 |
| 3 | Address top myth or mistake | Day 4 |
| 4 | Social proof or case study | Day 7 |
| 5 | Soft pitch or next-step CTA | Day 10 |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Offer too long or generic instead of solving one specific problem
- Requesting excessive form fields; ideal is name+email or email only
- Neglecting follow-up sequence after initial delivery
- Lead magnet topic unconnected to paid offerings
- Ignoring mobile optimization and email deliverability

## Promotion Channels
- Organic: blog content upgrades, social pinned posts, homepage banners, exit-intent popups, sidebar widgets
- Paid: Facebook/Instagram lead ads, Google Ads on problem-based keywords, newsletter swaps, podcast guest appearances
