# How to Create an Effective SEO Report in 2026

Source: https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-report/
Author: Carlos Silva
Published: 2026-04-10

## Core Premise
SEO reporting tracks organic performance and demonstrates ROI, now spanning both traditional search and AI search (AI Visibility).

## Definitions
- **SEO report**: overview of selected metrics illustrating a site's performance in regular search and AI search.
- **AI Visibility**: how often a brand appears in AI-generated answers (Google AI Overview, ChatGPT, etc.).
- **Authority Score**: Semrush's site-quality metric from backlinks, organic traffic, and other indicators.

## Steps
1. **Choose relevant metrics**: organic clicks, AI Visibility score + brand mentions, CTR, conversion rate, keyword rankings, backlinks/referring domains, site health.
2. **Gather data**: GSC (Performance > Search Results, export page-level clicks/CTR); AI Visibility Toolkit (AI Visibility score 1-100, brand mentions, citation counts); GA4 (Reports > Engagement > Landing page, filtered to organic); Organic Traffic Insights (de-anonymizes "not provided" keywords); Position Tracking (weekly rank updates); Backlinks tool (referring domains, new/lost links, Authority Score trend); Site Audit (140+ technical factors).
3. **Build report structure**: executive summary → traffic overview → AI Visibility data → keyword performance → conversions/ROI → backlink analysis → content performance → technical health → recommended actions.
4. **Presentation best practices**: logical structure aligned to business goals; explain *why* metrics changed; consistent cadence (weekly/monthly/quarterly); end on actionable positives, not just problems; use data visualizations.
5. **Automate**: Semrush My Reports — drag-and-drop widgets from multiple sources, scheduled PDF generation, email delivery.

## Data
10M marketing professionals use Semrush; monthly reporting is most common; reports deliverable as PDFs, slide decks, spreadsheets, or automated dashboards.
