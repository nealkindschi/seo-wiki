# Why Your Content Isn't Getting Cited: A GEO Diagnostic Checklist

Source: https://www.lumar.io/blog/best-practice/geo-diagnostic-checklist-ai-search/
Author: Lumar Editorial Team
Published: 2026-09-14

## Introduction

Publishing quality, relevant content that still fails to appear in generative AI search results is a common challenge. Ranking position alone won't necessarily tell you which underlying cause you're dealing with.

## Key Takeaways

- Poor generative visibility stems from multiple causes: candidate eligibility, semantic relevance, chunkability, or authority
- Page-level relevance doesn't guarantee passage-level retrieval
- Authority's importance varies by query type
- Diagnosis precedes optimization strategy

## The GEO Diagnostic Checklist

### 1. Candidate Page Eligibility
- Can you describe the page's primary topic in one sentence?
- Does the title reflect the topic and match query intent?
- Does the page stay focused or cover loosely related subjects?

Per Google's guidance, pages must be indexed and eligible for Google Search snippets, though meeting basic requirements doesn't guarantee generative AI inclusion.

### 2. Clear Aboutness Signals
- Is the core concept introduced early?
- Does opening content stand alone without relying on distant information?
- Do secondary sections support the main topic?

"Long introductions, extensive background information, or loosely related sections can make the page's main topic less clear."

### 3. Chunk Retrievability
- Does each section cover a focused topic?
- Are related information groups together?
- Is important information contained within coherent sections?

Google confirms generative features "use retrieval techniques to find relevant content from its Search index."

### 4. Standalone Passage Context
- Can passages be understood independently?
- Are references like "this" or "it" clear in isolation?
- Do passages include necessary entities, units, and constraints?

### 5. Authority Constraints
- Is the query sensitive or safety-critical?
- Are authoritative sources consistently cited?
- How does your authority compare to competitors?

Authority functions as "a filter, not a score," with variable influence depending on query characteristics.

### 6. Problem-Solution Alignment
- What specific problem are you addressing?
- Does your change directly relate to it?
- Are you making generic "AI-friendly" modifications without clear rationale?

"Improving one area of a page won't necessarily resolve an issue elsewhere."

## GEO Diagnostic Summary

1. Is the page a likely candidate?
2. Does it clearly communicate its topic?
3. Is information organized into retrievable passages?
4. Do passages contain standalone context?
5. Could authority limit visibility?
6. Do changes address the identified problem?
