# Semrush Academy: Content Marketing Courses (overview)

Source: https://www.semrush.com/academy/courses/content-marketing/

## Featured courses
- **Content Toolkit Crash Course** (Rita Cidre, 12 lessons/35 min) — building a future-ready content strategy with Semrush's Content Toolkit; AI-powered + traditional search discovery.
- **Content-Led SEO with Brian Dean** (28 lessons/5 hrs) — ranking on Google to drive traffic/sales via SEO-focused content strategy.
- **Content Marketing Essentials for SEO and AI Search** (Rita Cidre, 16 lessons/1 hr) — topic clusters, E-E-A-T-driven content, AI citation optimization; covers traditional search and AI platforms (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews).
- **Content Marketing Principles for Business** (Chima Mmeje, 6 lessons/1 hr) — researching, developing, and aligning content for a target audience.

## Core competencies taught
Content strategy planning, creation and promotion, audience targeting, storytelling, and SEO tactics integrated with content development.
