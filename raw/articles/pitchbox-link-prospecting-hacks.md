# Link Prospecting Hacks

- Source: Pitchbox Blog
- URL: https://pitchbox.com/blog/link-prospecting-hacks/
- Author cited within: Stewart Dunlop

## Core Link Prospecting Principles

A quality prospect requires mutual value — "links are a two-way street."
Evaluate prospects through five questions:

1. **Metrics standards** — domain authority, organic traffic, and health indicators
2. **Relevance** — topical alignment with your audience
3. **Content uniqueness** — does your pitch offer new, complementary information?
4. **Linking likelihood** — target blog posts and resource pages, avoid forums and sales pages
5. **Freshness** — prioritize recently published, frequently updated content

## Three Primary Prospecting Strategies

### Skyscraper 2.0
Beyond traditional Skyscraper prospecting of exact-match competitors:
- Step 1: mine backlinks from directly competing articles
- Step 2: expand to "slightly adjacent topics" with related keywords

Example: for a WooCommerce campaign promoting "How to create an online
store," target sites linking to dropshipping guides, side-hustle articles,
and Etsy tutorials. This adjacent-topic expansion yielded 213 additional
opportunities in the example given.

### Guest Posting
Avoid relying solely on search operators — they miss untapped sites and
attract fee-charging gatekeepers. Instead:
- Use long-tail keyword variations (3+ words)
- "Piggy-back" competitors' backlink profiles to find guest-posting opportunities
- Bridge to adjacent industries (e.g. targeting education blogs for a credit
  card company)

### Resource Page Link Building
Target educational, evergreen content by:
- Using search operators to locate resource pages
- Applying quality filters (minimum DR, organic traffic)
- Mining authority-site backlinks via Ahrefs filters for "links" or
  "resource" pages

## Tools Mentioned

- **Ahrefs** — keyword explorer, content explorer for opportunity identification
- **Pitchbox** — campaign creation, blogger outreach, automated prospecting with deduplication

## Key Takeaway

Automation plus quality filtering can "save literally hundreds of hours per
month" while maintaining prospecting standards.
