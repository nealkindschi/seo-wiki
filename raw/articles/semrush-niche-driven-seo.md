## Definition
Niche driven SEO is a strategy that helps sites with smaller audiences gain search visibility by optimizing for keywords tied to a specific market segment. Less competition than general SEO, but niche businesses must work harder to stand out.

## Benefits
- Low-cost, long-term: organic can't be bought, so a more level field than paid; slower but cheap-to-acquire sales.
- Visibility and conversions: cites Semrush State of Content Marketing (top 2023 goals: traffic, brand awareness) and Marketing Insider Group (paid + social < 10% of B2B website traffic, leads, sales).
- Trust and loyalty: E-E-A-T shapes content assessment; consistent niche visibility builds authority and emotional connection.

## Strategy steps
1. Understand the audience: GA4, social analytics, CRM, surveys, interviews, competitor traffic comparisons. Audience research cited as top content-marketer success factor.
2. Optimize the site: page speed (Portent conversion-by-load-time research), broken links, duplicate content, HTTPS, sitemap, clear structure; regular audits (errors, then warnings, then notices).
3. Identify target keywords: volume vs. keyword difficulty; long-tail = lower KD, fewer competitors, higher purchase intent; topic research for subtopics.
4. Create high-value content: match intent, original and in-depth, visuals, current stats and expert insight, on-page optimization. Start with a blog, expand to video/podcasts/tools. Examples: Pipedrive ("confirmation email template"), Freetrain (problem-based queries like "where to keep your phone at the gym").
5. Build relationships with influencers/publications: backlinks as votes of confidence (Backlinko: top pages have more); linkable assets, influencer discovery, engage before outreach, communities, events, newsworthy press releases.
6. Track metrics: organic traffic, backlinks, rankings, SERP visibility, CTR, time on page, bounce rate, Authority Score; conversions/leads are the core outcome; targets fit business maturity.
7. Monitor competitors: identify via keyword searches/tools, benchmark, find keyword/content/link opportunities.

## Conclusion
Build niche market share by grounding SEO in audience needs. Promotes Semrush trials and AI Narratives GA4 tool.
