# The Ultimate Guide to LLMO, GEO & AIO

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ultimate-guide-llmo-geo-aio-malte-landwehr-khqne/
Author: Malte Landwehr
Published: 2024-01-21

## Core Premise
LLMO (LLM Optimization) / GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) / AIO (AI Optimization) are near-synonymous terms for techniques to influence how LLM-based systems (Google SGE/Bard, ChatGPT, Bing Chat/Copilot, Perplexity, Claude, Llama 2, Gemini, PaLM 2, 50+ models) generate and cite content.

## Definitions
- **Co-occurrences**: strategically pairing your brand with target keywords/topics on authoritative sites to shape LLM associations.
- **RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)**: systems that run a traditional search to find/cite sources before generating a response.

## Tactics to influence LLM answers (brand/association-building)
- Target authoritative sites: heavily-moderated community sites (Wikipedia, Reddit, Quora), database platforms (Crunchbase, Yelp, IMDB), major editorial outlets (NYT, Bloomberg, Reuters, Forbes), and Google's Knowledge Graph source list (~63k sites).
- Publication strategies: paid placement (Forbes, Times of India), organic participation (Reddit/Wikipedia), press releases to major outlets.
- Avoid LLM-bot blocking: monitor robots.txt for accidental blocks of GPTBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, Anthropic-ai, omgilibot.

## Tactics to gain LLM traffic (content-level)
- **Structure**: one URL per subtopic (enables multiple citations per response); executive summaries for long content; pro/con lists; quotable UGC.
- **Technical**: rank in organic top 10 (50% of cited sources per an Onely/ZipTie study); minimize V8 compile time; server response <500ms; no crawl/index issues; content not JS-dependent; crawlable comments/reviews.
- **Quality**: include stats/quantitative data; quote/cite identifiable experts; authoritative but simple/fluent language; explain jargon; keep current; remove thin content; follow E-E-A-T.
- **Semantic optimization**: diverse vocabulary (TF-IDF/Word2Vec/LSI-aware); schema markup; structure categories matching LLM groupings; explicitly mention sources.

## Example
Elon Musk's "Tesla whistle" PR stunt — media co-occurrence of "Tesla" + "whistle" in training data led ChatGPT's autocomplete to associate the two, illustrating how co-occurrence shapes LLM associations.

## Limitations
Effectiveness varies by topic/LLM scale; models update at different speeds (SGE ~24h, some models never retrain); small niche sites can outperform dominant brands; training-data sources per LLM are unknown; no model is real-time updated.
