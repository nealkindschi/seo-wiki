# Broken Link Building

Source: https://www.semrush.com/blog/broken-link-building/
Author: Rachel Handley
Published: 2023-07-03

## Core Premise
Broken link building = finding dead/404 links on external sites and convincing admins to replace them with links to your relevant content. Benefits both parties (admin fixes their site, you gain a backlink); often higher success rate than cold outreach since it offers genuine value.

## Benefits
Earn backlinks from established pages; inherit links previously pointed at competitors' dead pages; generates content ideas via link-worthiness analysis.

## 5-Step process
1. **Identify your own broken backlinks** — Semrush Backlink Audit, filter for broken internal links (4xx/5xx) with non-toxic scores (0-44), export.
2. **Identify competitors' broken backlinks** — (a) Semrush Backlinks tool on competitor domains, filter broken pages; (b) Google search operators for resource pages ("useful links" + topic, `intitle:"useful resources"`), check links via browser extension.
3. **Evaluate link opportunities** — Authority Score (0-100), contextual relevance, Wayback Machine check of original content, likelihood of admin acceptance.
4. **Identify outreach contacts** — target specific staff writers, not generic addresses; use author bylines, social profiles, Prowly for contact discovery.
5. **Send the pitch** — concise, personalized, reference the broken link + replacement value + hyperlink; follow up after 3 business days.
