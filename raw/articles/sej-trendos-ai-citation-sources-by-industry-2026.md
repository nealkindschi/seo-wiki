# We Analyzed 107 Million AI Answers. Here's Where Each Industry's Citations Actually Come From

Source: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-search-citation-sources-trendos-spa/586289/
Author: Gintare Rimolaityte (COO & Co-Founder at Trendos)
Publish Date: 2026-08-27
Study Source: Trendos (sponsored research)

## Key Claims & Data

Analysis of 107 million AI-generated responses across four platforms:
ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. Sources
categorized into three groups:

1. **Community & UGC** (~50% across all industries): Reddit, YouTube,
   forums, Quora
2. **Brand/Retail/Owned** (varies by industry): Amazon, vendor sites,
   product pages
3. **Independent Editorial & Reference**: Media, Wikipedia, B2B
   directories

### Industry Breakdown (Community/UGC | Brand/Retail | Editorial/Reference)

- IT & Solutions Services: 51% | 2% | 47%
- Consumer Goods: 50% | 46% | 4%
- Communication Services: 50% | 10% | 40%

### Per-platform personality

- **Google AI Overviews**: "The most social. YouTube and Reddit
  dominate every industry, often four fifths of the top 10."
- **Perplexity**: Combines community platforms with directories and
  media.
- **ChatGPT**: "The most editorial. Wikipedia, trade media, and
  reference lead, with Reddit high on the list."
- **Gemini**: "The most industry dependent. Editorial and reference for
  services, but leans to brand and retail sites for physical products."

### Named domains

Beyond Reddit/YouTube/Wikipedia/Amazon: Clutch, G2, GoodFirms, Gartner,
SourceForge, Slashdot, PCMag, TechRadar.

### Universal finding

Reddit appears in the top three sources across all industries and
engines studied — described as the single most consistent citation
domain.

## Critical takeaways

- **B2B Software**: third-party proof (G2, Clutch, Gartner profiles,
  trade media) matters more than brand homepages.
- **Telecom/Media**: earned coverage dominates; maintain Wikipedia
  entries and pursue press placements over more landing pages.
- **E-commerce**: product pages and marketplace listings are primary
  citation sources and should be optimized as "answer copy."

## Methodology

Minimal disclosure: "107 million AI answers this year," top-10 citation
sources per engine/industry. No disclosed query count, date range, or
sampling methodology beyond that.
