# Link Building for SEO

- Source: Ahrefs
- URL: https://ahrefs.com/seo/link-building

## Core Definition

Link building involves acquiring hyperlinks from external websites to your
own pages, functioning as "votes" that signal authority to search engines
and improve rankings.

## The Four Link Building Approaches

1. **Adding Links** — manually placing links on social profiles, business
   directories, review sites, forums. Low effort, minimal SEO value, but
   useful "foundational links" for new sites.
2. **Asking for Links** — outreach-based: guest posting, skyscraper
   technique, resource page requests, broken link building, image
   attribution requests, HARO contributions, unlinked-mention conversion,
   PR-driven coverage.
3. **Buying Links** — paying for placements; risky due to Google's
   manipulation penalties and potential wasted spend on ineffective links.
4. **Earning Links** — organic backlinks from genuinely noteworthy content:
   proprietary research/data, original experiments, thought leadership,
   industry surveys, breaking news.

## Five Link Quality Metrics

- **Authority** — links from established, well-known sites carry more
  weight (Domain Rating and similar metrics measure this)
- **Relevance** — topic alignment matters (a health blog benefits more from
  fitness-source links than automotive), though sensible cross-topic links
  still work
- **Anchor Text** — the clickable link text; excessive optimization risks
  manipulation penalties, natural variation is preferable
- **Placement** — in-content links carry more weight than footer/sidebar
  placements, tied to click-through likelihood
- **Destination** — homepage links are easiest to acquire; "money pages"
  need authority transferred via internal linking from linkable assets

## High-Impact Strategies

- **Competitor backlink analysis** — study who links to competitors'
  homepages/best pages, pursue similar opportunities, use alerts for new
  competitor links
- **Targeted outreach** — find top-ranking pages for target keywords,
  identify their link sources, filter for relevance, personalize outreach
  (typical success rate ~5% from 100 emails)
- **Linkable assets** — surveys, research studies, calculators, rankings,
  tutorials, infographics, coined terminology
- **Content promotion** — advertising, influencer outreach, email lists,
  social platforms to maximize organic link accumulation
- **Guest posting** — pitch compelling ideas for underserved topics, or
  offer to improve underperforming existing content on target publications

## Key Statistics

- New websites benefit from "a few dozen foundational links" as a starting
  point
- Prior relationships dramatically improve outreach success rates
- Ahrefs' own ranking-factors study accumulated nearly 3,000 backlinks
  across 1,700+ domains
- Professional link builders report ~5% success rates on cold outreach

## Critical Success Factor

"People link to web pages that are interesting and useful." Genuine
industry relationships built before requesting favors substantially improve
acceptance rates. Authority sites with relevant content are the
highest-impact targets.
