## Concept
A niche market is a focused group of people/businesses wanting a specific offering, vs. mass market (many products/industries, hard to lead). Narrow scope, build around passion, serve an audience you understand.

## Benefits
Efficient marketing (fewer personas, smaller ad budget); stronger social proof and word of mouth; less competition (audience must still be large enough); higher loyalty (partner not vendor).

## How to find a niche
1. Reflect on passions and skills (what comes naturally, what friends ask your advice on).
2. Identify customers' problems and needs: buyer personas (demographics, spending, behavior), market research, talk to prospects; find a "space within the space".
3. Research competition (audiences, brand, appeal). Tools: Exploding Topics (emerging trends), Google Trends (interest over time), AnswerThePublic (question phrases -> sub-niches), Ubersuggest (KD, CPC), Google Keyword Planner, ClickBank, Amazon (best-sellers).
4. Define the niche and assess profitability: quality tier, price point, location, values, demographics; compare competitor prices (Amazon products, G2 software, agency directories services, PRICEFY.IO). Good niche = demand + few quality providers.
5. Test before scaling: simple site/landing page, trials or free samples, modest paid ads, crowdfunding. If results disappoint, refine rather than abandon.
6. Articulate a clear value proposition from customer + competitor research + personal experience.

## Examples
Georgetown Cupcake, The Container Store, Drybar, SoulCycle, Ties.com, BuddyCRM (CRM for construction/manufacturing), Dorm Mom, Kirrin Finch, Frostbeard Studio, Jacamo.

## Expert insights
Torokhtiy: let niche find you, avoid analysis paralysis. Chow: passion surfaces problems others share. Madan: intersection of expertise, passion, unmet gap; learn audience language. Bland: build around clients who get most value. Brandrup: share your "why". Lubanovic: blend passion with market research, sell where niche gathers (Etsy), continuous feedback.

## Takeaway
Specializing uses resources efficiently, delivers faster, builds loyalty. Niche can be short- or long-term strategy.
