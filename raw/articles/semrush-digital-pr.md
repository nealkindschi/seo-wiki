# Digital PR

Source: https://www.semrush.com/blog/digital-pr/
Author: Carlos Silva (contributor: Sydney Go)
Published: 2025-02-25

## Core Premise
Digital PR builds brand visibility/reputation through media coverage, high-quality backlinks, and positive digital mentions — combining SEO benefit with broader reputation management, distinct from plain link building.

## Benefits
Improved SEO via authoritative backlinks; increased referral traffic from trusted media; stronger brand authority via third-party validation; measurable/trackable, compounding value over time.

## 5 tactics with examples
1. **Data-driven research studies** — Asana's 2024 State of Work Innovation survey (13,000+ respondents, 6 countries) earned VentureBeat/Forbes coverage via original research.
2. **Creative campaigns** — Duolingo's chaotic-mascot TikTok campaign (séances, twerking, flash mobs) reached 15M+ followers.
3. **Newsjacking** — Surreal cereal capitalized on AI-copywriting-prompt trend with billboards, 1,400+ LinkedIn likes.
4. **Expert thought leadership** — services like Qwoted connect experts with journalists seeking commentary.
5. **Product-focused PR** — Apple iPad mini 7 launch generated coverage (e.g. ZDNet).

## 7-Step campaign framework
1. Set clear objectives (measurable goals, tactics, metrics, timeline).
2. Understand your audience (target publications, sales-team input, audience-analysis tools).
3. Analyze competition (patterns in coverage, backlink gap analysis for publications linking competitors not you).
4. Brainstorm newsworthy content — 61% of journalists want original research/reports from PR pros (Cision 2024).
5. Create unique content (compelling narrative, visuals/data viz, methodology, press kit).
6. Develop distribution strategy across owned/earned/paid channels; target relevant journalists.
7. Write compelling pitches — strong subject line, 3-sentence newsworthiness summary, specific stats, clear CTA.

## Monitoring
Google Alerts for mentions; sentiment analysis via social monitoring tools; track mention volume/reach/presence score; identify most influential linking sites.
