# What Are Topic Clusters? 6 Ways To Create Content Clusters

Source: https://surferseo.com/blog/topic-clusters/
Author: Petar Marinkovic
Updated: 2025-12-11

## Core Premise
Topic clusters (pillar + cluster pages + internal links) demonstrate subject-matter expertise to search engines and improve UX. Reflects the post-Hummingbird shift from keyword-focused to topic-focused SEO — search engines now weigh intent and concept relationships, tying into E-E-A-T.

## Definition
Three components: pillar page (comprehensive overview), cluster/supporting content (detailed subtopics), internal links connecting them.

## Keyword vs. topic distinction
Keywords = specific search terms placed strategically (titles, URLs, headers, meta, body). Topics = broader subjects encompassing multiple subtopics/keywords, guiding overall strategy.

## 7-Step process
1. **Pick topics from GSC data** — group existing content by theme; use Surfer's Domain Map to automate cluster identification and find coverage gaps.
2. **Start with seed topics** — align with business goals/audience needs ("what do you want to be known for?"); generate foundational keywords from products/services; expand via topical mapping tools.
3. **Use Google to find topical clusters** — Related Searches, Autocomplete, browser extensions (Keyword Surfer); prioritize informational/commercial over navigational queries.
4. **Create a pillar page** — typically 2,500+ words, clear H2/H3 hierarchy, table of contents, natural keyword placement in titles/headings/metadata, contextual internal links with descriptive anchors, lists/tables/images for readability.
5. **Create supporting cluster pages** — target buyer-journey-advanced users, one subtopic per page in depth, distinct keyword sets to avoid cannibalization (keyword overlap across pages with same intent, causing internal competition).
6. **Link related pages together** — all supporting pages link up to pillar; supporting pages link sequentially to each other (A↔B↔C) to optimize link-juice flow; descriptive anchor text, never "click here."
7. **Complete coverage and iterate** — identify gaps, avoid repetition, return to research as needed.

## Data/examples
- Shopify: "Ultimate Guide to Dropshipping" pillar + private-labeling/print-on-demand/niche-selection cluster.
- Dino Digital HR SaaS case study: 17x organic traffic increase from deep single-cluster coverage before expanding, using Surfer for topical mapping + AI content production.
- Build timeline: "a few weeks to a few months" to research and write a cluster correctly.
- No universal standard for cluster count/size.
