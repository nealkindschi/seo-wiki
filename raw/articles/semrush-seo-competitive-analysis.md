# SEO Competitor Analysis

Source: https://www.semrush.com/blog/how-to-do-seo-competitive-analysis/
Author: Carlos Silva
Published: 2026-05-29

## Core Premise
SEO competitor analysis examines how rivals perform in SERPs and increasingly on AI platforms (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT), informing content strategy, keyword targeting, and link building.

## Definitions
- **SEO competitor analysis**: analyzing competing sites' search + AI-platform performance.
- **Direct vs. organic competitors**: direct = same industry; organic = ranks for similar keywords despite a different industry (example: Men's Health magazine vs. Gymshark as an organic competitor).

## 8-Step process
1. **Identify competitors** — search 3-5 relevant keywords across Google/YouTube/Reddit/AI platforms; Domain Overview's "Main Organic Competitors"; distinguish direct vs. organic.
2. **Analyze traffic** — organic/branded traffic trends, top pages, multiple traffic sources (AI referrals, social, email, display), correlate algorithm updates with traffic changes.
3. **Analyze keyword footprints** — keyword gap analysis (terms competitors rank for that you don't), filter by intent (commercial/transactional) and position (top 10), segment into topic clusters via AI tools.
4. **Check AI visibility** — AI Visibility Toolkit cross-platform comparison; identify missing prompts where competitors appear but you don't; focus on business-relevant prompts.
5. **Reverse-engineer winning pages** — evaluate angle, audience targeting, H2 structure, format, title tags, authority signals (original research, frameworks, credibility indicators) on top-10 competitor pages driving traffic.
6. **Analyze backlinks** — Authority Score/referring-domain/link-volume trends; backlink gap analysis for sites linking competitors not you; identify content types attracting links.
7. **Explore off-site presence** — social posting frequency/engagement; Reddit/community `site:` searches; document sentiment, praised benefits, pain points.
8. **Review local SEO** (if applicable) — Google Business Profiles, review sentiment, local visibility, AI-platform presentation of local businesses.
