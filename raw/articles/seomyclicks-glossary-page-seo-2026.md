## Core argument
A glossary should be a topic-clustered "semantic hub", not an
alphabetical list of definitions. Claimed ranking mechanisms: long-tail
capture per definition, internal linking between entries, entity
reinforcement, and matching expanded search intents.

## Five-step process
1. Gather terms from Search Console, competitor pages, People Also Ask,
   and keyword tools.
2. Group terms by meaning (core terms, technical definitions, intent
   variations, problem-based queries).
3. Treat each term as an entity with related concepts.
4. Write each definition with: a clear explanation, a use-case example,
   related concepts, and links to deeper content.
5. Build internal links from glossary entries to blog posts, service
   pages, and other glossary terms.

## AI search
Glossaries can be cited by AI search tools, so definitions should be
concise, factual, and well structured.
