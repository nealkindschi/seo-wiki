# What Is AI Saying About Your Brand? Develop a Brand Canon and Find Out

Source: https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/brand-canon
Author: John Lovett, VP Analytics at Seer Interactive
Publish Date: 2026-08-17

## Definition

A **brand canon** documents what's factually true about your
organization and your desired market positioning. Unlike brand
guidelines (visual standards) or a style guide (language rules), it
serves as measurable ground truth for evaluating AI-generated
statements about your brand.

## Key claims

- Seer analyzed 28,123 responses across 6 AI models about their own
  brand: ~70% factual accuracy, with nearly 30% containing wrong,
  outdated, or declined (refused/no-answer) information.
- "Visibility without accuracy is being wrong at scale" — mentions mean
  little if the AI's description of the brand is incorrect.
- No standardized measurement currently exists that tracks both AI
  visibility *and* the accuracy of what's said, at the same time.

## Data & statistics

- 28,123 responses analyzed across 6 AI models.
- ~70% factual accuracy; ~30% inaccuracy/decline rate.
- (Aside, about Seer itself, not the study) 92% client retention rate.

## The three-layer framework

1. **The Canon (Specification)** — tracks true claims and desired
   positioning, each sourced and dated.
2. **Claim Inventory (Test Suite)** — converts the canon into
   falsifiable assertions, tiered by revenue impact.
3. **Accuracy Score (Test Run)** — measures assertion pass/fail rates
   across AI platforms.

## Five canon categories

| Category | Content | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Facts | Company details, leadership, products | Sourced |
| Desired positioning | 2-3 market claims to own | Declared |
| Brand description | Category, customers, elevator pitch | Declared |
| Differentiators | Capabilities and advantages | Both |
| Falsehoods | Retired products, old taglines, competitors | Both |

## Scoring dimensions

- **Factual correctness** — accuracy of stated facts.
- **Canon alignment** — alignment with intended positioning.
- **Attribution quality** — source credibility (owned vs. third-party
  vs. competitor).
- **Hallucination presence** — fabricated claims with no source basis.

## Recommended fixes

- **Retrieval fixes (weeks)**: update content, correct third-party
  listings, optimize for exact query matching.
- **Corpus fixes (quarters)**: build an authoritative web footprint for
  the next training cycle — you cannot directly edit training data.

## Key takeaways

- Most companies lack any documented ground truth against which to
  measure brand accuracy.
- Predicted failure modes (deprecated product names, old analyst
  reports) should be tracked proactively, not discovered reactively.
- Refresh the canon on a cadence to prevent stale benchmarking.
- Human review is required — model self-assessment isn't statistically
  reliable.
- Accurate visibility beats high visibility with low accuracy.
