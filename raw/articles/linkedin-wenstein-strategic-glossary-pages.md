# From Zero to Hero: Boosting SEO with Strategic Glossary Pages (paraphrase)

Argues glossary pages are an underused SEO asset: they educate visitors,
make technical content easier to understand, and position a site as an
authority.

## Why glossaries help SEO
- Naturally contain keywords, including long-tail phrases.
- Create many internal linking opportunities.
- Can lower bounce rate by keeping visitors engaged.
- Can attract backlinks.

## Building the glossary
A 15-step process covering audience research, keyword research, writing
clear definitions, internal linking, mobile responsiveness, multimedia,
promotion, and ongoing updates.

## Structure
Three formats compared:
1. Single page with anchor links.
2. Pages grouped by letter.
3. A separate page for each term — described as the best option for SEO.
Also recommends search functionality and accessibility.

## Structured data
- ItemList schema for the full glossary.
- DefinedTerm schema for individual term pages.

## Design and accessibility
Mobile-friendly; usable with screen readers and keyboard navigation;
possibly multilingual.

## Measurement
Track page views, time on page, and bounce rate (e.g. Google Analytics);
refine based on the data.

## Examples cited
Glossaries from Semrush, HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, SugarCRM.
