# SEO for Brand Awareness: A Complete Guide

Source: https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-brand-awareness/
Author: Yannick Weiler (contributors: Shannon Willoby, Bartłomiej Barcik)
Published: 2024-04-11

## Core Premise
SEO and brand awareness are mutually reinforcing: better search visibility drives recognition, which drives more branded searches and backlinks, which further improves SEO.

## Definitions
- **Brand awareness**: recognition from repeatedly encountering your site in search results.
- **Long-tail keywords**: specific, non-branded phrases with lower volume and lower difficulty.
- **Backlinks**: authority-vote signals from external domains.

## 5 tactics
1. **Create keyword clusters** — group related long-tail keywords on one topic (Keyword Strategy Builder) to rank for multiple terms with a single page, building topical authority faster than one-keyword pages. Example cluster: "personalized scented candles," "make your own candle scent," "custom scented candles."
2. **Determine content type** — analyze competitor top-ranking pages within each cluster for structure/title/heading/length/image patterns; match or exceed them.
3. **Create high-quality content** — SEO Writing Assistant for readability, SEO optimization, originality, tone-of-voice guidance.
4. **Build backlinks** — Backlink Gap tool vs. competitors; outreach; broken-link replacement and guest blogging as alternative tactics.
5. **Provide strong UX** — Core Web Vitals (Site Audit); HTTPS (Google prioritizes HTTPS results).

## Measurement
Branded search traffic (Organic Rankings tool, branded-keyword filter); brand mentions (Brand Monitoring app) tracking total/linked mentions.
