# How to Do Keyword Research in 2026

Source: https://www.semrush.com/blog/keyword-research/
Author: Chris Hanna (contributors: Faizan Ali, Alex Lindley)
Published: 2026-05-05

## Core Premise
Keyword research has shifted from volume-first to business-value-first, from Google-only to search-everywhere (Reddit, Amazon, ChatGPT, etc.), and from query-matching to prompt research (longer, conversational AI prompts).

## 6 Discovery methods
1. **Check existing search visibility** — GSC queries with high impressions/low clicks; Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance report for grounding queries; Semrush Organic Rankings filtered Position 11+, Volume 100+, Difficulty 0-49%.
2. **Mine first-party data** — sales-call questions/objections/language, support-ticket recurring issues, onboarding-conversation confusion points.
3. **Study social platforms/forums** — Reddit, Quora, YouTube comments, TikTok; paste raw comment text into ChatGPT/Claude to extract unanswered questions/pain points/keyword opportunities; target zero-volume keywords confirmed by real user demand.
4. **Search keyword databases** — Keyword Magic Tool for related terms; filter "Questions" for long-tail; long-tail keywords align with AI "query fan-out" (AI running multiple related sub-searches to build a comprehensive answer).
5. **Keyword gap analysis** — traditional (Keyword Gap tool, terms competitors rank for that you don't) and AI search (AI Visibility Toolkit's competitor-prompt "Missing"/"Weak" tabs).
6. **Analyze SERP features** — People Also Ask (matches conversational AI queries), Related Searches, AI Overviews (indicate topic coverage; low click potential if fully answered).

## Prioritization framework (6 dimensions)
Conversion potential (business actions over pure informational value) · Search volume · Click potential (high: comparisons/best-of/complex how-tos; low: definitions/simple facts) · Real-world demand signals (sales calls, Reddit, tickets, YouTube comments) · Trend analysis (favor stable/growing; declining is risky unless demand-backed or seasonal) · Attainability (new sites: difficulty 0-49%; established sites can go higher).

## Quick filter (before adding a keyword to the list)
Must satisfy at least one: builds brand awareness, improves AI visibility, attracts qualified leads, drives direct purchases.

## Key framing
"Keyword research is an ongoing process — not a one-time project."
