# Topic Clusters for SEO

Source: https://www.semrush.com/blog/topic-clusters/
Author: Alex Lindley
Published: 2025-10-29

## Core Premise
Topic clusters group interconnected, thematically related pages (one pillar + multiple subpages) to build topical authority, improve rankings, and increase visibility in both traditional search and AI/LLM tools.

## Definition
Pillar page = broad overview of a topic. Subpages/cluster pages = associated subtopics. All connected via internal linking.

## Benefits
- Topical authority (supports E-E-A-T)
- Expanded keyword + AI-prompt coverage
- Improved site structure (helps search engines, users, LLMs understand content relationships)
- Better UX/navigation

## 5-Step Process
1. **Choose a core topic** — brainstorm on brand relevance, past performance, existing content, audience interest, competitor coverage; broad enough for multiple pieces, focused enough to stay coherent; new sites should prioritize one topic first.
2. **Research topics** — keyword research tools for target keywords/prompts; evaluate intent, volume, difficulty; check trend stability.
3. **Identify pillar pages and subpages** — use AI keyword-strategy tools to auto-generate structure; examine keyword clusters (shared search intent); prioritize by intent/volume/difficulty.
4. **Create quality content** — pillar first, designed with cluster pages in mind; reciprocal internal linking; primary keyword in title/meta/URL/H1/opening paragraph; secondary keywords naturally; write for humans first; competitor content analysis.
5. **Measure performance** — track Google rankings (position tracking) and ChatGPT appearance; tag/group keywords by cluster; expand high performers, revise underperformers.

## Examples
- Healthline: "Everything You Need to Know About Allergies" pillar + symptom/cause/treatment subpages.
- Petcube: "Puppy Care 101" pillar + training/adoption/supplies subpages.
- Wistia: video marketing guide pillar + funnel-stage/example/promotion cluster pages, with embedded video.
