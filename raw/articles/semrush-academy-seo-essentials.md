# Semrush Academy: SEO Essentials with Semrush (course overview)

Source: https://www.semrush.com/academy/courses/seo-essentials-with-semrush/
Instructors: Kyle Byers (Director of Organic Search, Semrush), Rita Cidre (Head of Academy, Semrush)

## Structure (20 sections / 5 core areas)
1. Foundational strategy — SEO fundamentals, three key components.
2. Keyword research — identifying target keywords with Semrush tools.
3. On-page & technical SEO — content optimization, fixing technical issues.
4. Off-page SEO — backlinks and authority.
5. AI Visibility — brand presence in AI platforms (ChatGPT etc.).
6. Bonus: local SEO strategy + a real-world case study.

## Key concepts taught
The three pillars (on-page, off-page, technical); keyword research methodology (volume, difficulty, relevance, intent); site audit/error identification; link-building opportunities and competitive backlink analysis; expanding visibility beyond traditional search into AI platforms.

## Framework
Measure competitive visibility → research keywords → optimize on-page → audit technical issues → build backlinks → track AI visibility. Certificate on completion.
