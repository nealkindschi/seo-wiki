# How To Control What LLMs Say About Your Brand in Three Steps

**Last Updated:** May 19, 2026
**Author:** Eli Smith, Content Marketing Specialist, Siege Media

## Executive Summary

LLMs generate brand descriptions from consensus across third-party
sources rather than authoritative brand claims. Controlling brand
perception requires a "multi-vote" approach targeting the sources LLMs
cite — a three-step audit framework to measure and influence how AI
systems describe a brand.

## Key statistics

- ChatGPT drives 83% of LLM activity across Siege Media's tracked
  sites.
- Brands using proprietary data earn 45% more AI citations than those
  using generic "best overall" positioning.
- Specificity and originality outperform high-volume mentions.

**Visibility benchmarks**: Great = 75-80% visibility, 70-75% sentiment,
positions 1-2. Good = 55-75% visibility, 65-70% sentiment, positions
2-3. Needs Improvement = below 40% visibility.

## The three-step "multi-vote" audit

1. **Prompt audit** — run 10-20 buyer-intent queries across ChatGPT,
   Perplexity, and Gemini, capturing brand appearance/absence,
   descriptive language used, competitor positioning, and specific
   descriptor terms.
2. **Source audit** — categorize every third-party citation by type:
   best-of listicles, review platforms, Reddit threads, news/PR
   coverage, affiliate roundups, category-defining publishers.
3. **Descriptor audit** — extract and tally the exact phrases
   describing the brand across the top 5-10 sources to identify
   consensus gaps.

## Tactics

1. **Focus on descriptor consistency, not just mention count.** "Most
   organizations measure AI mentions when the right metric is
   descriptive consistency" across sources.
2. **Target specificity over generic positioning.** Generic "best
   overall" descriptions lack buyer-query anchors; specific positioning
   (e.g. "best for solo consultants") performs better.
3. **Timeline expectations**: 60-90 days to shift descriptor
   share-of-voice in earned media; listicles reindex 30-60 days after
   that.
4. **Share-of-voice target**: 55-80% across the top 10 sources for a
   given prompt.
5. **Descriptor discipline**: maintain consistent brand descriptors
   across owned and friendly third-party listings.

## Case study: Zapier

Anchored editorial around four descriptors (automation, workflows,
integrations, operational efficiency) — generated a $7.2M traffic
increase and a 290% lift in monthly organic traffic.

## Siege Media's own tracked visibility (example)

"best content marketing agency" — 96% visibility, position 1.6. "best
enterprise SEO agency" — 70% visibility, position 2.9.
