# Beginner's Guide to Link Building

- Source: Moz
- URL: https://moz.com/beginners-guide-to-link-building

## Core Definition

Link building is acquiring hyperlinks from external websites to your own.
Search engines use links to discover new pages and determine ranking
quality. It works alongside technical SEO, on-page optimization, content
quality, and user experience.

## How Search Engines Use Links

1. **Discovery** — links help engines crawl and index new pages
2. **Ranking signals** — link volume and quality from authoritative sites
   influence rankings

Google's PageRank algorithm treated links as "votes of confidence."
Manipulative tactics led to the Penguin updates (2012+), which penalize
low-quality link building and over-optimization.

## Quality Standard

Modern link building emphasizes "quality, relevance, and authenticity" over
spammy tactics, reflecting Google's increasing sophistication at filtering
poor-quality backlinks.

## Benefits Beyond Rankings

1. **Relationship building** — outreach creates long-term industry
   connections and establishes trust/authority
2. **Referral traffic** — high-quality links from relevant sites drive
   qualified visitors beyond ranking improvements
3. **Brand authority** — content-based link building demonstrates expertise
   and thought leadership

## Critical Success Factor

All link campaigns require "something of value to build links to."
Link-worthy assets (blog posts, tools, research, graphics) make earning
links achievable rather than forced.
