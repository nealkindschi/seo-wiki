# SEO Copywriting: 10 Tips to Boost Your Content Performance

Source: https://www.semrush.com/blog/seo-copywriting/
Author: Carlos Silva
Published: 2023-01-31

## Core Premise
SEO copywriting optimizes for both search engines and human readers simultaneously — rank for relevant keywords while genuinely engaging the target audience.

## 10 tactics
1. **Find the right keywords** — target specific, low-competition terms (Keyword Magic Tool); prioritize "Easy"-"Very Easy" difficulty initially.
2. **Find questions people ask** — "People Also Ask," AlsoAsked, AnswerThePublic, Quora, Semrush Topic Research.
3. **Identify and satisfy search intent** — 4 types (informational, navigational, commercial, transactional); match content format to intent.
4. **Check competitors' articles** — keyword gap analysis for terms competitors rank for that you don't; analyze competitor backlink profiles for content-format patterns.
5. **Create linkable assets** — original research, case studies, in-depth guides that exceed competitor quality to earn links naturally.
6. **Optimize headers/title tags/meta descriptions** — titles under 55-60 chars with keyword; unique meta descriptions under 120 chars with CTA; descriptive H1 + proper H2/H3/H4 hierarchy.
7. **Create organized, easy-to-read content** — clear structure, short paragraphs leading with the main idea (inverted pyramid/BLUF), simple vocabulary, avoid jargon; SEO Writing Assistant for readability scoring.
8. **Include visuals** — videos/images/charts/infographics; image SEO best practices (filenames, compression, alt text, structured data); increases time-on-page and shares.
9. **Include CTAs** — guide readers to logical next steps aligned with intent; place purposefully throughout.
10. **Use a table of contents** — clarifies structure, confirms the page meets user needs, keep headings specific/concise.
