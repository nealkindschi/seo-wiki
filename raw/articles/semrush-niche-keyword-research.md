## Core concept
Niche keywords are highly specific long-tail terms with little competition and high relevance ("sports shoes" = competitive; "carbon plate running shoe for heel striker" = niche). Claimed benefits: better visibility, more targeted traffic, higher conversion.

## Five steps
1. Determine target audience: GA4 Demographic details (Reports > User Attributes) for language, interests, location, age, gender.
2. Research the market: industry publications, podcasts, webinars; topics covered by multiple outlets or recurring over weeks/months; find subject-matter experts on LinkedIn.
3. Evaluate competitors: direct competitors with similar product/audience, not just same-industry; Keyword Gap for content gaps.
4. Browse online communities: Reddit, Quora, Facebook groups show specific questions, often after Google failed.
5. Compile and research preliminary keywords, then validate in tools.

## Seven tools
1. Semrush Keyword Magic Tool: 25B+ keywords; filter KD "Easy" / 0-49%; free accounts 10 searches/day.
2. Semrush Keyword Gap: your domain vs. up to four competitors; "Missing" and "Untapped" tabs; filter KD, position, volume 11-100.
3. Google Keyword Planner: PPC tool; sort by avg monthly searches low-to-high since low volume suggests niche terms.
4. Google Autocomplete: add qualifiers to seeds (e.g. "micro business", "non-profits").
5. Google Keyword Tool (free autocomplete scraper, 0-100 popularity).
6. Google Search Console: Performance > Queries for terms already earning impressions/clicks without deliberate targeting.
7. WordTracker: Google, Amazon, YouTube, eBay data; volume, competition, IAAT counts; Niche Explorer; paid, 7-day trial.
