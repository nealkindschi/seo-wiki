# How Volatile Are AI Responses?

**Author:** Glen Allsopp, Detailed
**Published:** 2026-09-23

## Methodology

- 28-day study of ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews.
- 1,300+ commercial prompts checked daily; 70,000+ total responses.
- Prompts were keyword-based, rewritten in a conversational style. Author
  notes they are imperfect and do not reflect follow-up conversations.

## Findings

- **Top brand stays visible.** The brand named most often for a prompt
  appeared on 82% of days in ChatGPT and 89% in AI Overviews. Picking the
  leader from the first 14 days only still gave 79% (ChatGPT) and 85%
  (AIO) presence over the following 14 days.
- **Top spot is narrow.** Gap between first and second brand is typically
  ~2 days out of 28. The leader changed between the two fortnights on
  roughly half of prompts.
- **Stable core, churning tail.** Brands named on >=80% of days ("core")
  changed ~13% day to day in ChatGPT and ~11% in AIO. The remaining
  brands ("tail") changed 78% (ChatGPT) and 65% (AIO) of the time. Cited
  domains showed the same pattern. Only about half of ChatGPT prompts had
  a core brand at all, usually one or two names.
- **Cited URLs churn, cited types don't.** ~25% of ChatGPT's cited pages
  were cited again the next day vs. ~50% for AIO. Articles were ~60% of
  citations on both. Content-type mix shifted only a few points on a
  typical day.
- **Video citations declined.** Video was ~14% of AIO citations in week 1
  and ~8% in week 4.
- **First position less stable than presence.** The usual leader was
  named first on 50% of days in ChatGPT (present on 67%) and 60% in AIO
  (present on 78%).
- **Identical answers are rare.** For two random days, the same brand list
  appeared 0.3% of the time (ChatGPT) and 1.1% (AIO). Same cited-domain
  set: 2.4% (ChatGPT), 0.3% (AIO). Author notes the test is strict: a
  near-identical list counts as a miss.
- **Some prompts never repeat.** 41% of ChatGPT prompts never returned
  the same brand set on any pair of days, vs. 30% in AIO.
- **Leading domain cited often, rarely first.** The most-cited domain
  appeared on 82% of ChatGPT days and 89% of AIO days, but was cited
  first on only 43% and 28% of days respectively.

## Takeaway

Individual answers vary a lot, but the most prominent brands and domains
persist. Exact-match tracking is unreliable; track presence and share
over time instead.
