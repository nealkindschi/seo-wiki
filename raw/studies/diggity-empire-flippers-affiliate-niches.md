# What a Data Dump From Empire Flippers Told Us About the Best Niches for Affiliate Marketing

Source: https://diggitymarketing.com/best-niches-for-affiliate-marketing/
Author: Matt Diggity (Diggity Marketing), data from Empire Flippers
Date listed: 2025-08-06 (article images date from 2021)
Ingested: 2026-10-25
Note: saved from a WebFetch summary (paraphrase, not verbatim).

## Study
300+ affiliate, display-ad, and Amazon Associate sites sold via Empire Flippers since 2019; grouped by niche, assets, monetization model.

## Findings
- Highest adjusted revenue: News & Education ~$8,439/mo. Religion & Spirituality raw figure higher but skewed by one huge site; niches with too little data removed (Religion & Spirituality, Jewelry, Lifestyle, Agriculture).
- Lowest revenue: Baking ~$773/mo (low capital to launch, so not necessarily unprofitable).
- Best content ROI: Medical ~$33.14 revenue per published page.
- Best link ROI: Office ~$5.61 revenue per backlink (remote-work growth).
- Best time ROI: Survival & Security ~$2,860/hour (author warns outlier + self-reported hours).
- Monetization: affiliate sites avg ~$5,540/mo, ~3x more per backlink than other models with fewer links; Amazon Associates higher per page ($7.95) but author still ranks affiliate stronger.
- Backlinks: diminishing returns after a few hundred; most sites 250-750. Content: most sites 25-250 pages; value per added page drops past ~250. Time: unreliable, not charted.

## Niche highlights (monthly revenue / pages / backlinks)
Medical ~$4,591 / ~138 / ~1,146 (competitive, often needs authority site). Home ~$2,675 / ~349 / ~5,412 / ~4.9 hrs/wk. Pet Care ~$4,319 / ~1,247 / ~4,984. Outdoors ~$3,126 / - / ~35,724. Automotive ~$3,326 / ~302 / hard links. Electronics ~$2,055 / ~377 / ~1,226. Technology ~$3,362 / ~452 / ~12,878 (high-ticket). Sports ~$2,946 / ~260 / ~2,513. Health & Fitness ~$1,678 / ~437 / ~3,366. Food & Beverages ~$4,039 / ~561 / ~2,603. Personal Finance: high-commission offers (loans, debt relief, investing).

## FAQ
Affiliate niche = focused segment with shared traits/purchasing habits. FAQ names Home & Kitchen best for revenue/multiple sales.

## Caveats (per fetch)
Only sold sites (survivorship bias); small samples and outliers; self-reported time; promotional for Diggity Marketing and Empire Flippers; internal inconsistency (quick summary: Home & Kitchen, Fitness & Sports, Health & Personal Care top three; body favors Medical).
