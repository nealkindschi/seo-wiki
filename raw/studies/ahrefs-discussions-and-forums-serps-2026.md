# Analysis: Reddit Appears in 83.9% of Google's Discussions and Forums Results

Source: https://ahrefs.com/blog/discussions-and-forums-serps/
Author: Glen Allsopp (Head of Marketing Strategy & Research, Ahrefs)
Contributors: Xibeijia Guan
Reviewer: Ryan Law
Published: 2026-08-27

## Key Claims & Statistics

**Reddit's Dominance:**
- Reddit appears in 83.9% of worldwide Discussions and Forums SERP results
- In the US specifically, Reddit shows in 87.8% of instances
- Average of 1.29-1.31 links per SERP appearance
- Most prevalent in 34 countries globally

**Top Three Sites Account for Majority:**
- Reddit, Facebook, and Quora comprise 76.75% of all discussion links
- Top 10 domains represent 84.87% of all appearances
- Top 1,000 domains account for 96.81% of total mentions

**Ranking of Top Platforms:**
1. Reddit.com - 21.77M SERP appearances
2. Facebook.com - 9.84M appearances
3. Quora.com - 8.22M appearances
4. JustAnswer.com - 2.1M appearances
5. Mayo Clinic - 839.4K appearances

**Facebook's Growth:**
- Appears in 38.3% of discussion feature results
- Overtook Quora in January 2026
- Public Facebook Groups (launched Oct 2010) drive all appearances

**Query Intent & Length:**
- 98.7% of appearances target informational queries
- Feature appears in 11.7% of US SERPs overall
- More common for longer queries: rare at 1 word (0.3%), scales to 3x frequency at 8 words

**Domain Characteristics:**
- Average domain age: 21 years, 8 months
- Youngest prominent domain: 3 years, 11 months old (XDAforums.com)
- Average Domain Rating of top 1,000 sites: 58.3
- Moderate correlation (ρ=0.38) between domain authority and prominence

**Geographic Distribution:**
- Most prevalent in: US (11.7%), Canada (10.7%), Philippines, Australia, UK
- Reddit dominant in 34 countries; Quora prominent in India (#2); Facebook strong in South Africa (#2)

**URL Concentration:**
- 27 domains rely on single URLs for 10%+ of appearances
- 768 of top 1,000 domains depend on one URL for 1%+ of presence
- No correlation found between backlinks to specific threads and SERP rankings

## Methodology

Examined 145M US Google search results; the Discussions and Forums
feature appeared in 16.93M (11.7%) of them. Used Ahrefs' proprietary web
index across dozens of countries; domain registration dates
cross-referenced via archive.org where needed.

## Key Takeaways

1. Extreme concentration among discussion platforms limits diversity in
   search results.
2. Facebook's recent growth signals platform shifts in user-generated
   content discovery.
3. Domain authority matters less than assumed — newer sites can rank if
   providing relevant discussions.
4. Query sophistication increases feature prevalence (longer, more
   specific searches).
5. Engagement strategies should focus on authentic community
   participation rather than promotional tactics.
6. No backlink correlation suggests Google ranks discussion threads on
   topical relevance and community engagement metrics, not traditional
   link signals.
