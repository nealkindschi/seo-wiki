# TOFU Is Back: The 50% Visibility Test for Top-Funnel Content

**Last Updated:** July 9, 2026
**Author:** Taylor Lynch, Content Marketing Specialist, Siege Media

## Executive Summary

Top-of-funnel (TOFU) content remains viable in the LLM era, contrary to
2024 predictions of its demise — the shift is that AI models now
recommend specific brands alongside definitions, not just define terms.
Success depends on "brand visibility": appearing in at least 50% of an
AI model's responses to a given query. Only topics where a brand can
realistically hit that threshold, while retaining meaningful search
volume, warrant continued TOFU investment.

## The brand-visibility mechanism

LLMs increasingly name specific vendors when answering category
questions rather than staying purely definitional. Example: HubSpot
appears in 83% of Gemini responses to "what is CRM?" — a fundamentally
different behavior than traditional search ranking.

## The 50% test

Two conditions must both hold for a topic to qualify for TOFU budget:
1. Brand achieves at least 50% visibility across multiple LLM
   platforms.
2. The topic carries sufficient search volume to justify the
   investment.

**Qualifying topics**: product-connected or category-defining questions
where the answer inherently references tools/vendors (e.g. "what is
CRM?").

**Disqualifying topics**: purely definitional/encyclopedic questions
answerable without commercial references (e.g. "what is
photosynthesis?").

## Competitive dynamics

Category leaders don't dominate every prompt in their space. Challenger
brands can compete in sub-category queries, under-served topics, and
emerging entity associations — e.g. "CRM for real estate teams" or
"CRM with best mobile app" offer separate ranking opportunities from
the flagship category term.

**Platform variation is large**: HubSpot shows 83% visibility on Gemini
for "what is CRM?" but doesn't appear at all in Google's AI Overview
response to the same query — Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Oracle NetSuite
rank there instead.

## Methodology: the "re-index method"

A repeatable weekly process:
1. Sample existing TOFU topics across relevant AI models, multiple runs
   per prompt.
2. Measure brand mention rate and flag topics at or above 50%
   visibility.
3. Cross-reference flagged topics against search-volume data.
4. Prioritize investment/deprioritization decisions accordingly.
5. Repeat as a recurring pulse check, not a one-time audit.

## Tactics for winning model recommendation

1. **Own category definitions** — provide the clearest, most complete
   explanation of the category itself.
2. **Demonstrate solutions in practice** rather than sales-focused
   copy.
3. **Optimize for model accessibility** — clear definitions, structured
   headers, direct answers.
4. **Build entity associations** through affiliate partnerships and
   editorial coverage.
5. **Establish authority through depth** in topic coverage.

## Recommendations

- Shift budget toward topics meeting the 50% visibility threshold
  rather than pursuing all TOFU opportunities indiscriminately.
- Run structured prompt sampling to defensibly score visibility across
  platforms.
- Monitor visibility weekly — scores shift with model updates and
  competitive content.
- Prioritize sub-category and problem-framed queries where incumbents
  have less dominance.

## Terminology used

- **Brand visibility**: how frequently an AI names a brand when
  answering a question.
- **Citation vs. recommendation**: a model referencing a source page
  (citation) vs. naming a brand directly (recommendation).
- **Entity associations**: connections a model forms between a brand
  and specific concepts over time.
