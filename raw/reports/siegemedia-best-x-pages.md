# What Best "X" Posts Actually Deliver + Why Investment Matters

**Last Updated:** June 30, 2026
**Author:** Marianna Mankle, Content Strategist, Siege Media

## Executive Summary

Benchmark study of 101 "best X" posts across 12 B2B websites (financial
software, HR/IT SaaS, communications, fintech, fleet tech). BOFU
listicle content delivers well-above-average engagement and measurable
ROI when properly structured and positioned.

## Methodology

- Google Analytics Data API sampling, 12 months (March 2025-Feb 2026).
- 101 B2B "best X" pages across 12 sites; titles matching "best
  [category]" intent, minimum 50 sessions in the window.
- Excludes "best practices"/"best ways to" content, gated webinars,
  community Q&A, non-English URLs, product-page taglines,
  brand-named-alternative posts.
- Stated limitation: "a meaningful but not exhaustive sample" — treat
  as benchmarks, not absolutes.

## Key findings

- **Median engagement rate: 61.1%**; 43% of posts exceed 65%; top
  quartile >69%, bottom quartile <52%. Median bounce rate: 38.9%.
- **Median session duration: 113 seconds**; top performers exceed 180
  seconds. Highest-traffic tier (2,000+ sessions): ~110s median;
  lowest tier (<500 sessions): 85s median.
- **Median pages per session: 1.0** overall; top-performing site
  category averages 2.73 — a large architecture-driven gap.
- Engagement distribution: 76%+ (11% of dataset), 61-75% (39%), 46-60%
  (23%), 31-45% (19%), <30% (9%).
- "Best X" content attracts commercial-intent readers primed for
  conversion, evidenced by GA4 engaged-session signals (10+ second
  sessions, conversion events, or 2+ page views).
- **Session duration is the primary quality signal.** Longer sessions
  correlate with deep comparison tables, transparent pricing context,
  and same-page answers to follow-up questions.
- **Audience-content alignment drives bounce rate**: mismatched traffic
  sources show bounce rates exceeding 70%; well-aligned content
  performs much better.
- High impression volume compounds returns without added spend — even
  modest CTR generates meaningful brand awareness among actively
  evaluating buyers when multiplied across high-impression pages.

## Recommended structure

- **In-body linking**: deliberate links from each "best X" entry to
  the relevant product/service page — treat each entry as a mini
  conversion funnel.
- **Comparison table elements**: "best for" callouts (the highest-
  weight decision column — readers scroll back to these most), clear
  feature/spec display, direct answers to "which is right for me?"
- Solve the query genuinely and completely; prioritize freshness and
  transparent pricing/feature info; build linking pathways from BOFU
  pages to product pages.
- Sites at or below ~1.08 pages/session should implement deliberate
  in-body linking and consistent cross-entry linking patterns.

## Business implications

BOFU investment compounds: organic visibility accumulates without added
media spend, CTR improvements from ranking/link-building compound, and
messaging reaches active buyers during evaluation. The pages-per-
session gap (1.0 median vs. 2.73 top performer) suggests large
optimization potential through architecture alone.
