# AEO/AI Search & Citation Statistics 2026

**Publication Date:** Friday, August 7, 2026
**Author:** Carrie Rose, Founder, Rise at Seven

## Executive Summary

AI search has become the primary answer layer for modern queries. With
nearly 900 million weekly ChatGPT users and Google's AI Mode surpassing
1 billion monthly users, AEO has moved from experimental to essential.
68% of US Google searches end without clicking a traditional result,
and AI citations concentrate heavily on specific domains (top 15
capture 68% of all citations). Despite 70% of marketers believing AEO
will impact strategy within 1-3 years, only 20% have begun implementing
it — a significant first-mover gap.

**Methodology note**: statistics synthesized from 22+ studies and
platforms (academic research, platform announcements, clickstream
analysis firms), spanning 2024-2026 — this is a compilation/roundup
article, not primary research.

## AI search user adoption

- ChatGPT weekly active users: 900 million (up from 400 million in Feb
  2025).
- ChatGPT monthly active users: 1 billion (as of June 2026).
- ChatGPT daily prompts: ~2.5 billion.
- Google AI Mode monthly users: 1 billion+ (surpassed in 2026).
- AI Mode query growth: more than doubled each quarter.
- AI search traffic growth (Q1 2025→Q1 2026): 42.8% increase (15.6B→
  27.4B visits) vs. Google Search's 2.4% growth over the same period.
- ChatGPT's market share of generative-AI traffic declined from ~76%
  to ~53% (2025→2026) as Gemini climbed past 25%.

Sources: OpenAI, Similarweb, Reuters, Google I/O 2026.

## Zero-click and click-suppression behavior

- 68% of all Google searches end without a click.
- Zero-click rate with an AI Overview present: ~83%.
- Zero-click rate in Google AI Mode specifically: 93%.
- User click rate when an AI summary appears: 8% (vs. 15% without).
- Source-link clicks inside AI summaries: only 1% of visits.
- Users ending their session after an AI summary: 26% (vs. 16%
  without).
- AI Overviews appear on 20%+ of Google searches; BrightEdge measured
  ~48% trigger rate on tracked queries (Feb 2026).
- Question-based queries get an AI summary 60% of the time (Pew
  Research); 1-2 word queries only 8% of the time.
- CTR suppression at organic position 1: 58% reduction (Ahrefs, Dec
  2025) — up from 34.5% reduction measured in an earlier Ahrefs study
  (April 2025).

Sources: SparkToro/Similarweb, Pew Research Center, BrightEdge, Ahrefs.

## AI referral traffic to websites

- ChatGPT referral traffic grew 206% YoY (Jan 2025 vs. Jan 2026).
- Domains receiving ChatGPT referrals grew from 71,000 to 260,000.
- Referral concentration: 30% of all referrals go to just 10 domains.
- ChatGPT referrals jumped 36.7% in a single month (May 2026, an
  all-time high).
- AI referral share of total traffic: ~1.08% average (range 0.1%-2.8%),
  growing ~1 percentage point per month.
- Retail-site traffic from AI referrals grew 693% YoY over the 2025
  holiday season.
- AI-referred visitors convert 31% better than non-AI traffic.

Sources: Semrush/Datos, SE Ranking, Conductor, Adobe Digital Insights.

## Citation patterns and domain concentration

- #1 most-cited domain overall: Reddit. Top 5: Reddit, YouTube,
  LinkedIn, Wikipedia, Forbes.
- Top 15 domains capture 68% of all consolidated AI citations.
- YouTube's share of social citations doubled from 18.9% (Aug 2025) to
  39.2% (Dec 2025).
- Reddit's share of social citations fell from 44.2% (Aug 2025) to
  20.3% (Dec 2025) over the same window.
- Wikipedia's share of ChatGPT responses crashed from ~55% (August) to
  under 20% (September) after a single algorithmic adjustment.
- Government sites are cited 6% of the time in AI search vs. 2% in
  traditional search.
- Perplexity concentrates 46.7% of its top-10 citation sources on
  Reddit.
- 83% of AI citations for commercial queries come from content updated
  within the past 12 months; 60%+ from content updated within 6 months.
- AI bot crawlers target recent content heavily: ~65% of crawls hit
  content under 1 year old.

Sources: Peec AI, 5WPR Citation Source Index, Goodie AI/Bluefish/
Emberos, Semrush, Pew Research, AirOps, Profound.

## Content optimization effectiveness (GEO/AEO)

- Maximum visibility lift from targeted optimization: up to 40%.
- Quotation addition: +37% visibility improvement.
- Statistics addition: +22% visibility improvement.
- AI citation "halo effect": cited brands get +35% more organic clicks.
- Early AEO adopters show 3.4x more AI visibility than late adopters.

Sources: Princeton/Georgia Tech/IIT Delhi GEO study (Aggarwal et al.,
ACM KDD 2024), Contently analysis, RevvGrowth, The Stacc.

## Marketer adoption and perception

- 70% of marketers believe AEO will impact strategy within 1-3 years.
- Only 20% are currently implementing it — a 50-point adoption gap.

Source: Acquia/Researchscape survey (n=500+).

## Market forecasts

- Gartner (Feb 2024): traditional search volume to decline 25% by
  2026.
- Semrush: AI to overtake traditional search by 2028.

## Key takeaways (author's own)

1. Citation concentration mirrors historical power-law distributions —
   top-15 domains command 68% of citations, a winner-take-most dynamic.
2. Platform citation preferences vary significantly: ChatGPT favors
   encyclopedic content, Perplexity concentrates on Reddit, Google AI
   Overviews distribute more broadly.
3. Citation volatility is high — Wikipedia dropped from 55% to under
   20% in a single algorithmic adjustment; YouTube citations doubled in
   4 months.
4. Freshness is a ranking factor — 83% of commercial-query citations
   come from recently-updated content.
5. Evidence-rich content formats win — quotations and statistics
   demonstrably boost citation visibility.
