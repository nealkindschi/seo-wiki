# The Content That Predicts B2B AI Traffic Most: Versus Pages

**Last Updated:** June 1, 2026
**Author:** Ross Hudgens, Founder & CEO, Siege Media

## Executive Summary

Analysis of 116 B2B websites to determine which content templates drive
AI search traffic (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Meta
AI). **Versus (vs) pages consistently outperform alternatives pages and
"best X software" listicles as predictors of AI search referrals.**

## Methodology

- 116 B2B GA4 properties, trailing 90 days ending May 24, 2026.
- Comparison-page definition: URLs containing "vs," "alternative," or
  "best…software"; minimum 50 sessions in the window; deduped per site,
  false positives removed.
- Statistical methods: Spearman rank correlation (primary, robust to
  heavy-tailed distributions) and Pearson correlation on log-
  transformed values for plotted subsets — both confirmed the same
  directional effect.
- Limitation: 53 of 116 properties returned zero comparison pages (real
  gaps plus URL-pattern blind spots); regex may have missed
  non-standard comparison content.

## Key findings

- **Versus pages: Spearman correlation of 0.65** with AI search
  traffic — double the next-best content type. Structural reason: LLMs
  prefer citing pages that match the exact "X vs Y" query intent, while
  alternatives/"best X" content competes directly with G2, Capterra,
  and affiliate sites for the citation.
- **Volume effect, with diminishing returns past 20 pages**: sites with
  1-5 comparison pages are the baseline; 6-20 pages → 350% increase in
  median AI sessions; 21+ pages → 900% more median AI sessions than the
  1-5 group. "The curve climbs steadily through every bucket."
- **Content explains only ~28% of the variance** in B2B AI search
  traffic; the remaining ~72% comes from brand authority, category fit,
  and citation-graph density. One in five top-20 sites studied had
  fewer than five transactional pages — brand authority alone can
  substitute for content volume.
- **Pricing pages are a proxy, not an independent driver**: a raw 21x
  difference disappeared once controlling for overall content
  investment. 91% of sites with pricing pages also built versus pages;
  only 37% of sites hiding pricing built versus pages — pricing
  presence reflects sales motion, not a standalone AI-traffic lever.
- **Multi-locale programs underperform**: massive multi-locale
  libraries generate fewer AI sessions than page counts would predict
  (smaller regional search volume, lower international AI adoption) —
  though alternatives content specifically drives 20-55pp higher
  engagement internationally, a counterpoint worth testing locally.

## Traffic composition

Over the 90-day window, 116 B2B properties generated 739,492 total AI
search sessions. ChatGPT dominates as the primary referral source;
Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Meta AI together contribute
~20% of volume. Claude usage is rising but currently lags in referral
attribution.

## Tactical recommendations

1. **Start with versus pages** — the strongest single-page-type signal
   in the dataset; recommended as the first sprint for a comparison
   program built from zero (acknowledged as more contentious/harder to
   greenlight than "best X"/alternatives content, which offers less
   internal friction).
2. **Build to reasonable scale** — the 350% lift between 1-5 and 6-20
   pages justifies investing in a comprehensive program; don't build
   irrelevant comparisons just to hit a page count.
3. **Layer alternatives and "best X" content after the versus
   foundation** — incremental signal once versus pages are established;
   total comparison count determines the top tier, with versus content
   leading the contribution.
4. **Set honest expectations**: content alone moves about a third of
   the variance; going from 5 to 21+ pages yields a realistic 900%
   median lift forecast, but category leadership needs brand authority
   *and* content, not content alone.

## Strategic implications

- **Prompt-tracking opportunity**: create a "competitive comparison"
  prompt category to track sentiment shifts between your brand and
  competitors; use comparison articles to plug gaps and lift net
  sentiment, improving the odds of winning the final LLM-generated
  comparison.
- **Challenger brands**: content is the most executable lever — 20-50
  transactional pages are achievable; established category recognition
  is not.
- **Established brands**: transactional libraries compound on top of
  existing brand authority; sites combining significant content volume
  with category authority rank highest in AI search traffic.
