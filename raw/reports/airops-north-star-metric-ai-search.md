# Brand Visibility Is the North Star for AI Search

**Publication Date:** July 31, 2025
**Author:** Josh Spilker (AirOps)

## Executive Summary

Traditional SEO metrics (clicks, pageviews, rankings) are argued to be
obsolete in an AI-driven search landscape where agents and answer
engines dominate decision-making. Marketing teams should prioritize a
"Brand Visibility Score" — how frequently a brand appears in
AI-generated answers relative to total answer volume for the category —
over traffic-focused metrics. "AI doesn't click. It answers."

## Key Findings

### The core problem
- Brands not appearing in AI-generated responses are effectively
  invisible to buyers.
- A widening gap between impressions and clicks as AI answers replace
  traditional search results.
- Teams optimizing metrics disconnected from revenue outcomes lose
  internal credibility.

### AI search adoption rates
- January 2025: AI Overviews in <5% of Google results
- July 2025: AI Overviews in 25% of Google searches
- "Current state": 50% of all U.S. Google queries include AI-generated
  summaries
- OpenAI processes roughly one query per American, per day
- ~60% of U.S. Google search queries influenced by AI

### The traffic collapse
- Traditional clicks are shrinking; discovery increasingly happens
  without attribution.
- High-intent traffic that remains carries substantially more value.
- Brands gaining citations in AI responses are the ones winning
  visibility.

## Brand Visibility Score framework

**Formula:** Answers mentioning your brand ÷ Total answers for your
category.

Incorporates:
- Share of voice relative to competitors
- Citation rate across platforms
- Sentiment analysis of mentions

**Supporting metrics:**
1. **Citation Rate** — % of LLM answers mentioning or linking to your
   brand.
2. **Sentiment Score** — (Positive mentions + 0.5 × Neutral mentions) ÷
   Total mentions.
3. **Share of Voice** — % of total citations vs. competitors.

**What it measures:** presence across AI agents (ChatGPT, Gemini,
Perplexity, Claude); frequency of human name-checking in decision
threads; brand-to-answer association reliability; credibility signals
that precede a site visit.

## Why legacy metrics fail

- **Pageviews** — inflated by bots, bounces, misaligned clicks.
- **Traditional rankings** — don't guarantee surfacing in AI interfaces.
- **CTR** — irrelevant amid zero-click answer dominance.
- **Leads/MQLs** — often vanity metrics without revenue attribution.
- "CMOs relying on traffic dashboards are going blind." — Guillaume
  Cabane, Hypergrowth Partners.

## Execution strategy: five-pillar approach

1. **Strategy — set the visibility agenda.** Define priority queries,
   topics, buyer moments; benchmark competitive presence. Key
   questions: Where is your brand currently cited? Where should you
   appear but don't? Which agents mention or ignore your brand?
2. **Metrics — track pipeline-driving indicators.** Replace traffic
   dashboards with metrics reflecting actual buying-journey patterns.
3. **Execution — build for agents, not just audiences.** Three
   tactical phases:
   - *Audit*: track brand surfacing in LLM answers/AI Overviews;
     identify citation gaps, sentiment shifts, competitive mentions.
   - *Planning*: prioritize high-intent mid-funnel topics where active
     evaluation occurs; organize into clusters supporting visibility
     and trust.
   - *Build*: refreshed mid-funnel content optimized for surfacing;
     modular, reusable answer blocks for agent extraction; off-site
     influence plays (Reddit, glossaries, citations).
4. **Team structure — move from headcount to leverage.** Recommended
   roles: Content Engineers (build extractable, schema-rich content
   blocks); Content Strategists (own coverage tracking and competitive
   positioning); Growth Engineers (maximize visibility efforts); staff
   with rapid-iteration capability.
5. **Growth — visibility-triggered actions.** When visibility metrics
   decline, trigger automated workflows: Create Content (address
   questions where competitors appear but you're absent); Refresh
   (update pages to compete in co-mention scenarios); Outreach (contact
   third-party sites securing competitor mentions); Community Building
   (foster authentic engagement establishing thought leadership).

## Specific recommendations

- Focus on extractable, agent-friendly formatting over volume.
- Develop "point of view" content only your brand can answer.
- Ensure content is structured, extractable, and refreshable for LLM
  consumption.
- Target bottom-funnel, high-intent questions where evaluation occurs.

**Platform priorities**: Google AI Overviews; ChatGPT, Gemini,
Perplexity responses; Reddit threads and comparison forums; glossary
hubs and expert roundups; evaluation-checklist resources.

**Competitive intelligence**: systematically track which agents surface
competitors while omitting your brand, to prioritize content/outreach
responses.
